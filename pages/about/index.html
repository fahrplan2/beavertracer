What is this?

Beaver Tracer is a learning tool that helps you understand how computer networks work — step by step.

It gives you a simplified practice network: enough to learn the important ideas, without all the confusing real-world noise. Even though the network is simplified, Beaver Tracer uses real protocol data — so what you learn here transfers directly to real networks.

What can I do with it?

  • Build your own network and connect devices
  • See how devices communicate with each other
  • Explore protocols and packets step by step
  • Capture and read network traces like a professional

Not sure where to start? Check the Help page.

The built-in Tracer

Beaver Tracer includes a packet analyser based on Wireshark technology. You can inspect real packets and learn how a professional tool looks and feels — in a friendlier, guided environment.

Why a Beaver? 🦫

Beavers don't wait for things to happen — they get to work. They build, log by log, until a dam stands where there was only a stream.

That's exactly what you do here: packet by packet, connection by connection, until a whole network makes sense. Patient, methodical, curious. A very beaver kind of skill.

For Teachers

Beaver Tracer was developed specifically for use in the classroom. The deliberately simplified network makes it possible to teach core networking concepts clearly and accessibly — without overwhelming learners with unnecessary detail.

Accompanying teaching materials are currently in development.

Have fun exploring — and good luck tracing! 🙂
