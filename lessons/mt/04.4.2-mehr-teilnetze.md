# Aktar subnets, octets oħra

Kuluri bħal fit-taqsima ta' qabel: [[n|network part]] · [[e|estensjoni]] · [[h|host part]]

## Subnets jew hosts — trid tiddeċiedi

Kull bit addizzjonali ta' estensjoni jirdoppja n-numru ta' subnets — u jnaqqas bin-nofs id-daqs ta' kull subnet. Għal network `/24` dan jidher hekk:

| Subnets | Prefix length | Netmask | Indirizzi għal kull subnet | Hosts għal kull subnet |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | `/25` | `255.255.255.128` | 128 | 126 |
| 4 | `/26` | `255.255.255.192` | 64 | 62 |
| 8 | `/27` | `255.255.255.224` | 32 | 30 |
| 16 | `/28` | `255.255.255.240` | 16 | 14 |
| 32 | `/29` | `255.255.255.248` | 8 | 6 |
| 64 | `/30` | `255.255.255.252` | 4 | 2 |

Meta tippjana, għalhekk, trid tiċċekkja t-tnejn: hemm **biżżejjed subnets** — u huma **kbar biżżejjed** għall-apparati kollha?

:::quiz mc
Network `/24` għandu jinqasam f'8 subnets. F'żona waħda hemm 40 apparat. Jaqbel?
- [ ] Iva, kull subnet għandu 62 hosts
- [x] Le, kull subnet għandu 30 hosts biss
- [ ] Iva, kull subnet għandu 254 hosts
:::

:::

## Il-ħarsa qasira: id-daqs tal-blokk

Jekk fhimt il-metodu fil-binary system, jista' jsir ukoll aktar malajr. In-numru ta' indirizzi għal kull subnet jissejjaħ **id-daqs tal-blokk**. Jirriżulta min-numru ta' host bits fl-octet fejn tinsab il-fruntiera — jew sempliċement bħala **256 naqqas l-octet tal-maskra**:

- `/26` → maskra `255.255.255.192` → 256 − 192 = **64** → subnets f'.0, .64, .128, .192
- `/27` → maskra `255.255.255.224` → 256 − 224 = **32** → subnets f'.0, .32, .64, .96 …

## Eżempju: tmien subnets

`192.168.42.0/24` f'**tmien** subnets: 3 bits (2³ = 8), minn `/24` isir `/27`, maskra `255.255.255.224`, daqs tal-blokk 32.

| Subnet | EEE | Network address | Broadcast address | Range għal hosts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [[e|000]] | `192.168.42.0` | `192.168.42.31` | `.1` – `.30` |
| 2 | [[e|001]] | `192.168.42.32` | `192.168.42.63` | `.33` – `.62` |
| 3 | [[e|010]] | `192.168.42.64` | `192.168.42.95` | `.65` – `.94` |
| 4 | [[e|011]] | `192.168.42.96` | `192.168.42.127` | `.97` – `.126` |
| 5 | [[e|100]] | `192.168.42.128` | `192.168.42.159` | `.129` – `.158` |
| 6 | [[e|101]] | `192.168.42.160` | `192.168.42.191` | `.161` – `.190` |
| 7 | [[e|110]] | `192.168.42.192` | `192.168.42.223` | `.193` – `.222` |
| 8 | [[e|111]] | `192.168.42.224` | `192.168.42.255` | `.225` – `.254` |

## Meta l-fruntiera tkun f'octet ieħor

Il-metodu jaħdem bl-istess mod meta n-network oriġinali jkun akbar minn `/24`. Allura l-estensjoni sempliċement tkun f'octet ieħor — u **l-octets kollha warajha** jappartjenu għalkollox lill-host part.

### Eżempju: `172.20.0.0/16` f'erba' subnets

2 bits ta' estensjoni, minn `/16` isir `/18` (maskra `255.255.192.0`). L-estensjoni issa tinsab fil-bidu tat-**tielet** octet, ir-raba' octet huwa kompletament host part:

<pre class="bits-block">
[[n|172.20.]] [[e|E E]] [[h|H H H H H H . H H H H H H H H]]
</pre>

| Subnet | EE | Network address (host bits 0) | Broadcast address (host bits 1) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [[e|00]] | `172.20.0.0` | `172.20.63.255` |
| 2 | [[e|01]] | `172.20.64.0` | `172.20.127.255` |
| 3 | [[e|10]] | `172.20.128.0` | `172.20.191.255` |
| 4 | [[e|11]] | `172.20.192.0` | `172.20.255.255` |

Attenzjoni: il-broadcast address tas-subnet 1 hija `172.20.63.255` — mhux `172.20.63.0`. Fir-raba' octet il-bits kollha huma host bits u għall-broadcast address jiġu ssettjati għal 1. Kull subnet għandu 2¹⁴ − 2 = 16 382 hosts.

### Eżempju: `10.16.0.0/12` f'tmien subnets

3 bits ta' estensjoni, minn `/12` isir `/15` (maskra `255.254.0.0`). Din id-darba l-fruntiera tinsab fit-**tieni** octet. In-numru 16 fil-binary huwa `0001 0000`; l-ewwel erba' bits diġà jappartjenu għan-network part, imbagħad jiġu t-tliet bits ta' estensjoni u host bit wieħed:

<pre class="bits-block">
[[n|10. 0 0 0 1]] [[e|E E E]] [[h|H . H H H H H H H H . H H H H H H H H]]
</pre>

| Subnet | EEE | it-tieni octet (network address) | Network address | Broadcast address |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [[e|000]] | [[n|0001]] [[e|000]] [[h|0]] = 16 | `10.16.0.0` | `10.17.255.255` |
| 2 | [[e|001]] | [[n|0001]] [[e|001]] [[h|0]] = 18 | `10.18.0.0` | `10.19.255.255` |
| 3 | [[e|010]] | [[n|0001]] [[e|010]] [[h|0]] = 20 | `10.20.0.0` | `10.21.255.255` |
| 4 | [[e|011]] | [[n|0001]] [[e|011]] [[h|0]] = 22 | `10.22.0.0` | `10.23.255.255` |
| 5 | [[e|100]] | [[n|0001]] [[e|100]] [[h|0]] = 24 | `10.24.0.0` | `10.25.255.255` |
| 6 | [[e|101]] | [[n|0001]] [[e|101]] [[h|0]] = 26 | `10.26.0.0` | `10.27.255.255` |
| 7 | [[e|110]] | [[n|0001]] [[e|110]] [[h|0]] = 28 | `10.28.0.0` | `10.29.255.255` |
| 8 | [[e|111]] | [[n|0001]] [[e|111]] [[h|0]] = 30 | `10.30.0.0` | `10.31.255.255` |

:::warning Żbalji tipiċi
- **Prefix miżjud ħażin:** Għal 4 subnets jiżdiedu 2 bits, mhux 4 — jgħodd in-numru ta' bits, mhux in-numru ta' networks.
- **Octet ħażin:** L-estensjoni tkompli direttament mal-network part eżistenti. B'`/16` tinsab fit-tielet octet, mhux fir-raba'.
- **Jintnesew l-octets ta' wara:** Jekk il-fruntiera mhijiex fl-aħħar octet, l-octets li jmiss kollha huma host part — għalhekk fil-broadcast address huma `255`.
- **Network address jew broadcast address jingħataw lill-apparati:** Ir-range tal-hosts jibda indirizz wieħed *wara* n-network address u jispiċċa wieħed *qabel* il-broadcast address.
:::

:::
