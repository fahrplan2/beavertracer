# Troubleshooting tan-network tal-iskola

:::sim
url=/sims/lesson-4.4.3.btsim
:::

Kollega waqqaf il-PCs fin-network tal-iskola — minn dakinhar kważi xejn ma jaħdem bejn l-oqsma. Hekk *suppost* tidher in-network:

| Apparat | IP address | Prefix length | Gateway |
|---|---|---|---|
| PC Verwaltung | `192.168.10.2` | `/26` | `192.168.10.1` |
| PC Lehrerzimmer | `192.168.10.66` | `/26` | `192.168.10.65` |
| PC Computerraum | `192.168.10.130` | `/26` | `192.168.10.129` |

Sib l-iżbalji u rranġahom.

<details>
<summary>Suġġeriment 1: Minn fejn nibda?</summary>

Ħares lejn l-:fa-gear: **IP Settings** ta' kull PC u qabbilhom man-network plan. Mhux kull żball jidher mill-ewwel.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Suġġeriment 2: Xi inhu eżattament ħażin?</summary>

Ikkalkula għal kull PC: f'liema subnet tinsab l-address tiegħu tassew? Jista' jkun li hija n-network address jew il-broadcast address? U n-netmask hija korretta?
</details>

:::task
title: In-network tal-iskola tranġa?
Iċċekkja l-addresses tat-tliet PCs u jekk jistgħux jaslu wieħed għand l-ieħor permezz tar-router. Is-simulazzjoni trid tkun qed taħdem fil-Modalità ta' tħaddim.
check: ip(9, "192.168.10.2/32")
check: ip(11, "192.168.10.66/32")
check: ip(13, "192.168.10.130/32")
check: pingOk(9, 11)
check: pingOk(9, 13)
check: pingOk(11, 13)
:::

## X'kien qed jiġri?

:::quiz match
PC Verwaltung -> L-address .70 tinsab fis-subnet tal-Lehrerzimmer — il-gateway .1 ma jistax jintlaħaq minn hemm
PC Lehrerzimmer -> In-netmask kienet /24 minflok /26
PC Computerraum -> L-address .191 hija l-broadcast address tas-subnet
:::

:::evaluate
Iċċekkja t-tqabbil
:::

:::tip Punt importanti
Żbalji tipiċi fis-subnetting: **address barra s-subnet tiegħek stess** (u allura anke l-gateway ma jistax jintlaħaq), **netmask żbaljata** u **n-network address jew il-broadcast address bħala address ta' apparat**. Oqgħod attent: l-IP Settings jaċċettaw valuri żbaljati bħal dawn mingħajr ebda twissija — il-kalkolu trid tagħmlu int.
:::
