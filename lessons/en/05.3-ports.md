# Ports: many connections at once

:::goal
**Learning goal:** You can explain how ports tell several connections on one computer apart, name the most important well-known ports and find typical port faults.
:::

:::sim
url=/sims/lesson-5.1.btsim
:::

In section 5.1 you saw: the browser and the TCP client send their packets to the same IP address — and still every packet ends up at the right service. The difference was made by the numbers `80` and `5000`, the **ports**. Now you'll look at how they work in more detail.
