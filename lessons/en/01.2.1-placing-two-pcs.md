# Placing and cabling two PCs

Here's how:

1. Switch to :fa-pencil: **Edit** mode.
2. Select the :fa-desktop: icon in the toolbox on the left and click twice on the workspace to place two PCs.
3. Select the :fa-link: tool, click the first PC and then the second — the two are now connected.

:::warning
You can tell an existing connection by the line between the devices. Connected it wrong? Use the :fa-ban: tool to remove the cable again and redraw it.
:::
