# Layer 2: Switches and local networks

:::goal
**Learning goal:** You can explain how a switch forwards frames based on MAC addresses, and find and fix typical faults in a local network.
:::

:::osi 2
:::

So far, only two devices were ever connected directly with a cable. But a real network — at home, at school, in a company — has many devices: PCs, laptops, printers, servers. On the workspace you see a typical small example: three PCs all connected to one **switch**.

This chapter is about **layer 2**, the data link layer: the local network, MAC addresses and the devices that distribute frames within it.
