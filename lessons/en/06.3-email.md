# E-mail: SMTP, POP3 and IMAP

:::goal
**Learning goal:** You can describe the way of an e-mail from sender to recipient, tell the jobs of SMTP, POP3 and IMAP apart, explain the role of the MX entry and find typical e-mail faults.
:::

:::sim
url=/sims/lesson-6.3.btsim
:::

E-mail needs several protocols at once. On the workspace you see two domains, each with a mail server:

| Person | Address | Mail server |
|---|---|---|
| **Anna** | `anna@beaver-schule.de` | **Mailserver Schule** (`mail.beaver-schule.de`) |
| **Ben** | `ben@biber-mail.de` | **Mailserver Biber** (`mail.biber-mail.de`) |

On top there's a **DNS-Server** that is responsible for both domains.
