# BeaverTracer — Course

Welcome to the **interactive course** for BeaverTracer. Here you learn step by step how networks work — right in your browser, with real protocols and a built-in packet analyzer based on Wireshark technology.

You need **no prior knowledge**. Every lesson starts with a small experiment: you build something, watch what happens in the network, and only then learn the technical terms for it.

## What to expect

1. **Introduction** — the interface, your first network, a web page request under the microscope
2. **Models** — how a packet is structured and how protocols are sorted into layers (ISO/OSI, TCP/IP)
3. **Switches and local networks** — how a switch knows where a packet has to go; VLANs and Wi-Fi
4. **Routers and IP addresses** — netmask, gateway and **subnetting**: dividing networks sensibly
5. **Transport layer** — ports, TCP and UDP
6. **Applications** — DHCP, DNS, HTTP and e-mail
7. **The home network** — router, NAT and everything from the previous chapters together
8. **Security** — who can listen in, encryption, certificates and HTTPS
9. **Firewall** — who is allowed into the network and who isn't
10. **VPN** — connecting two sites securely
