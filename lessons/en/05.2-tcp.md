# Observing TCP

:::goal
**Learning goal:** You can find in the tracer where TCP does the four jobs of the transport layer, show a TCP connection as a sequence diagram and work out sequence and acknowledgement numbers.
:::

:::sim
url=/sims/lesson-5.1.btsim
:::

The **Transmission Control Protocol** (**TCP**) is behind almost everything on the internet that has to arrive reliably: web pages, e-mails, downloads, remote maintenance via SSH.

On the next pages you observe a single TCP connection from start to finish — and look for yourself where TCP does the connection setup, reliable transmission and connection teardown.
