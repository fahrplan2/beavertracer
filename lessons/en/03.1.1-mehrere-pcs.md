# Connecting several PCs to a switch

:::sim
url=/sims/lesson-3.1.1.btsim
:::

On the workspace there's a switch and three PCs: **Anna**, **Ben** and **Clara**. Their IP addresses are already set (`192.168.0.10`, `.11` and `.12`) — only the cables are missing.

## Cabling

1. Switch to :fa-pencil: **Edit** mode and choose the :fa-link: **Link** tool.
2. Click **Anna** and then the **Switch**. A selection with the switch's ports (1 to 8) appears — choose **1**.
3. Choose :fa-link: again and connect **Ben** to port **2** and **Clara** to port **3**.

Every cable now shows a small number: the switch port it's plugged into.

:::tip Key point
A **switch** is a distributor with many connections, the **ports**. All devices connected to a switch together form a **local network** (*Local Area Network*, **LAN**).
:::

## Testing

Switch to :fa-play: **Run** mode, open the :fa-terminal: **Terminal** on **Anna** and ping Clara:

```
$ ping 192.168.0.12
```
