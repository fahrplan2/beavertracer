# Placing and connecting two PCs

Here's how:

1. Switch to :fa-pencil: **Edit** mode.
2. In the toolbox on the left, choose the :fa-desktop: icon and click twice on the workspace to place two PCs.
3. Choose the :fa-link: tool, click the first PC and then the second — the two are now connected.

:::warning
You can recognise an existing connection by the line between the devices. Connected the wrong way? The :fa-ban: tool removes the cable again and you can draw it anew.
:::

:::tip Key point
A **network** consists of at least two devices that are connected to each other and can exchange data.
:::
