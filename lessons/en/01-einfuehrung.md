# Introduction to BeaverTracer

BeaverTracer is a simulation in which you build networks yourself, put them into operation and follow their traffic packet by packet — without any real hardware.

In this first chapter you get to know the tool itself: the interface, the three working modes, and you build your first small simulation. At the end you load a web page and see how much actually happens in the background — the starting point for everything that comes after.

:::note
The chapters build on each other. Take your time with this first chapter — using the tool is the foundation for everything else.
:::
