# The three modes

BeaverTracer has three modes, which you can switch between using the three buttons on the far left of the toolbar:

- :fa-pencil: **Edit** — this is where you build your network: placing devices, wiring them up, configuring them. The simulation does not run while you do this.
- :fa-play: **Run** — the simulation starts. Devices boot up, connections become active, and you can, for example, send a ping from a PC's terminal.
- :fa-magnifying-glass: **Trace** — shows you every single packet traveling over your connections, including its exact contents.

:::note
You can switch back and forth between the modes at any time — nothing gets lost.
:::
