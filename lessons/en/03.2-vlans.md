# VLANs: one switch, several networks

:::goal
**Learning goal:** You can explain what VLANs are for, set up VLANs on a switch and connect VLANs across several switches with a trunk.
:::

In a school, the administration PCs are often connected to the same switch as the PCs in the computer room. Convenient — but should students really be able to access the computer in the school office directly, where grades and addresses are stored?

You could buy a separate switch for the administration and lay separate cables. But there's an easier way: you divide *one* switch into several networks that are separated from each other — into **VLANs**.
