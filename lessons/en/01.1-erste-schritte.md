# Interface and modes

:::goal
**Learning goal:** You can find your way around the BeaverTracer interface and switch between the three modes.
:::

:::sim
url=/sims/lesson-1.2.3.btsim
:::

There is already a small network of two PCs on the workspace. You'll try out the interface on it right away.

## The interface

- **Toolbar at the top** — this is where you switch modes and control the simulation.
- **Toolbox on the left** — only visible in Edit mode. Here you choose which device to place next (PC, switch, router, …) or whether you want to draw a cable.
- **Workspace in the middle** — your actual network. Scroll with the mouse wheel to move the view; hold **Ctrl** (Mac: **Cmd**) and use the mouse wheel to zoom. Alternatively, move the view while holding the space bar or the middle mouse button.
- **Lessons panel on the right** — you're reading it right now.

## The three modes

BeaverTracer has three modes, switched with the three buttons on the far left of the toolbar:

- :fa-pencil: **Edit** — this is where you build your network: place devices, connect them with cables, move them around. The simulation doesn't run meanwhile.
- :fa-play: **Run** — the simulation is running. You can open devices and, for example, type commands in a PC's terminal.
- :fa-magnifying-glass: **Trace** — shows you every single packet that went over your connections, with its exact content.

## Try it out

1. Click :fa-pencil: **Edit**. The toolbox appears on the left. Drag **Computer 1** to another spot with the mouse — the cable moves along.
2. Zoom in and out with **Ctrl** + mouse wheel. :fa-expand: **Fit** (bottom right of the workspace) fits the view exactly to your network again.
3. Click :fa-play: **Run**. The toolbox disappears. Now click **Computer 1**: its window with apps opens, just like on a real computer. Close it again with **×**.
4. Click :fa-magnifying-glass: **Trace**. This is where the recorded packets will end up later — the list is still empty.

:::tip
Prefer a guided round? Click the BeaverTracer logo at the top left and choose **Tour** in the main menu. The lessons panel closes — afterwards, **Lessons** at the top right brings you back here.
