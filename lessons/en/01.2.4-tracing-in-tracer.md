# Tracing in the tracer

Now switch to :fa-magnifying-glass: **Trace** mode. There's nothing to see here yet — click **+** in the top right and select your connection to observe it.

Now you'll see a list of all the packets that have traveled over it — including the pings from before. Click on one of the packets: on the right you'll see its exact structure, layer by layer.

:::tip
Feel free to look at several packets. A ping always consists of a request (Echo Request) and a reply (Echo Reply) — can you find both directions in the list?
:::

With that, you've gone through the basic principle of BeaverTracer once from start to finish: build, run, trace. In the next section, things will get a bit more complex.
