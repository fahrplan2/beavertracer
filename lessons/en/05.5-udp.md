# UDP: the big simplification

:::goal
**Learning goal:** You can compare UDP with TCP and explain for which applications UDP is the better choice.
:::

:::sim
url=/sims/lesson-5.5.btsim
:::

TCP does a lot — connection setup, acknowledgements, retransmission, order. But that costs time and extra packets. The second important protocol of the transport layer does without almost all of it: the **User Datagram Protocol** (**UDP**). Its packets are called **datagrams**.
