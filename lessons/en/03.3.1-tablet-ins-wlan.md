# Bringing a tablet into the Wi-Fi

:::sim
url=/sims/lesson-3.3.1.btsim
:::

The **Schul-PC** (school PC, `192.168.0.10`) and an **Access Point** are connected to the switch. The tablet **Anna** (`192.168.0.30`) is supposed to join the school Wi-Fi.

## Finding the Wi-Fi

Switch to :fa-play: **Run** mode and click the **Access Point**. It shows the name of its Wi-Fi network, the **SSID**: `Schule` (German for "school").

The access point works like a bridge: whatever arrives at it over the air, it passes on to the switch over its cable — and the other way round. That way Anna and the school PC end up in the same local network.

## Connecting the tablet

1. Click **Anna**, open the :fa-gear: **IP Settings** and there the **WiFi** tab.
2. Under **SSID**, enter the name of the Wi-Fi network: `Schule`, and click **Apply**.
3. Open the :fa-terminal: **Terminal** on Anna and ping the school PC:
   ```
   $ ping 192.168.0.10
   ```

Watch the workspace during the ping: radio waves spread out around Anna and the access point.

:::tip Key point
A **WLAN** (Wi-Fi) is a local network over radio. The **access point** connects the Wi-Fi to the wired network. Every Wi-Fi network has a name, the **SSID** (*Service Set Identifier*) — a device has to know the right SSID to connect.
:::

- [ ] Nothing — upper and lower case don't matter
- [x] Anna is no longer connected — the SSID has to match exactly

:::
