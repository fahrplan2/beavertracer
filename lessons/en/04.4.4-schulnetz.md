# Dividing up the school network

:::sim
url=/sims/lesson-4.4.2.btsim
:::

The school has the address range `192.168.10.0/24`. The administration (**Verwaltung**), the staff room (**Lehrerzimmer**) and the computer room (**Computerraum**) are each to get a subnet of their own. That's three networks — but equal-sized subnets only come in powers of two. So you divide into **four** subnets; the fourth is kept as a reserve (for example for a Wi-Fi network later).

## Step 1: Calculating

:::quiz table
| Extension bits | Prefix length | Netmask |
|---|---|---|
| {2} | /{26} | {255.255.255.192} |
:::

:::quiz table
| Subnet | Area | Network address | Broadcast address |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Administration | {192.168.10.0} | {192.168.10.63} |
| 2 | Staff room | {192.168.10.64} | {192.168.10.127} |
| 3 | Computer room | {192.168.10.128} | {192.168.10.191} |
| 4 | Reserve | {192.168.10.192} | {192.168.10.255} |
:::

## Step 2: Setting it up

The router is already set up: in every subnet it has the **first usable address** (`192.168.10.1`, `192.168.10.65` and `192.168.10.129`).

Now set up the three PCs. Every PC gets the **second usable address** of its subnet, the matching prefix length, and as its gateway the router's address in its subnet.

1. Switch to :fa-play: **Run** mode.
2. Open the :fa-gear: **IP Settings** of **PC Verwaltung**, enter the **IP**, **Prefix Length** and **Gateway** and click **Apply**.
3. Do the same on **PC Lehrerzimmer** and **PC Computerraum**.
4. Use `ping` to test whether the PCs can reach each other.
