# Introduction to BeaverTracer

BeaverTracer is a simulation tool that lets you build networks yourself, bring them into operation, and trace their traffic packet by packet — without any real hardware.

In this first chapter, you'll get to know the tool itself: the interface, the three working modes, and you'll build your first small simulation. At the end, you'll load a web page and see how much actually happens in the background — the starting point for everything that follows.

:::note
The chapters build on one another. Take your time with this first chapter — knowing how to use the tool is the foundation for everything else.
:::
