# BeaverTracer — Learning course

Welcome to the **interactive course** on BeaverTracer. Here you'll learn step by step how networks work — directly in your browser, with real protocols and a built-in packet analyzer based on Wireshark technology.

The course is aimed at **beginners with little or no prior knowledge** and takes you from the basics all the way to advanced topics such as routing protocols, VPN, and network security.
