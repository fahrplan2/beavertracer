# Dibadda ilaa gudaha: port forward

:::sim
url=/sims/lesson-7.2.3.btsim
:::

Hadda home network-ka waxaa ku jira **Heim-Webserver** u gaar ah (`192.168.178.10`). Clara waxay rabtaa inay bogga ka furto meel fog — laptop-keeda **Clara** wuxuu ku xiran yahay internet-ka dhanka midig.

## Dhibaatada

1. U beddel habka :fa-play: **Run**, kana fur `http://203.0.113.2/` :fa-globe: **Browser**-ka **Clara** — taasoo ah ciwaanka dadweynaha ee home network-ka.
2. Browser-ku wuu sugayaa oo sugayaa. Ka dib dhawr ilbiriqsi riix **Stop**.
3. Habka :fa-magnifying-glass: **Raad** ku fur trace u gaar ah **Heimrouter**, ee port-ka `wan0`.

:::quiz mc
Maxaa ku dhaca connection setup-ka Clara (`[SYN]` ee port 80)?
- [x] Wuxuu gaaraa home router-ka — laakiin router-ku ma gudbiyo, mana jawaabo
- [ ] Wuxuu gaaraa Heim-Webserver, kaasoo ku jawaabaya RST
- [ ] Marnaba ma gaaro home router-ka
:::

:::evaluate
Hubi jawaabta
:::

Packet ka yimaadda dibadda ma laha gelin NAT table-ka ku jira: qofna home network-ka ku jira ma bilaabin xidhiidhkan. Home router-ku ma ogaado device-ka uu u diro — sidaas darteed wuu tuuraa.

## Dejinta port forward

Adigoo adeegsanaya **port forward** (router-yada qaar waxaa lagu magacaabaa *Portfreigabe*) waxaad go'aamisaa: wax kasta oo ka yimaadda WAN port gaar ah wuxuu aadayaa device gaar ah oo ku jira home network-ka.

1. Guji **Heimrouter**, kuna fur **Tab**-ka **Port FW**.
2. Samee xeer: protocol `TCP`, WAN port `80`, LAN IP `192.168.178.10`, LAN port `80`, kadibna guji **+ Add**.
3. **Clara** ku fur mar kale `http://203.0.113.2/`.

:::task
title: Heim-Webserver ma laga heli karaa dibadda?
Waxay hubisaa in Clara ka samayn karto internet-ka xidhiidh la port 80 ee ciwaanka dadweynaha ee home network-ka. Simulation-ku waa inuu u socdaa Habka Socodsiinta.
check: tcpOpen(12, "203.0.113.2", 80)
:::

:::warning
Port forward wuxuu dalool u furaa gudaha: qof kasta oo internet-ka ku jira wuxuu gaari karaa device-ka ka dambeeya — oo ay ku jiraan weeraryahanno si toos ah u raadiya port-ka furan. Sidaas darteed gudbi kaliya waxa runti loo baahan yahay, device-kana ha ahaado mid cusub (updated). Wax dheeraad ah ka akhri Cutubka 9 (Firewall).
:::

:::tip Qodob muhiim ah
Dibadda, qofna ma bilaabi karo xidhiidh device ka danbeeya NAT — router-ku ma ogaado meesha uu u diro. **Port forward** wuxuu go'aamiyaa: packet-ka yimaadda **WAN port** gaar ah wuxuu aadayaa **LAN IP** gaar ah (iyo LAN port). Sidaas ayaa server-ka ku jira home network-ka looga heli karaa internet-ka.
:::
