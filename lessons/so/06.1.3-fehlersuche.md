# Cilad-raadin: Bogga Kontakt waa maqan yahay

:::sim
url=/sims/lesson-6.1.3.btsim
:::

Xafiiska maamulka ayaa soo wargeliyay: „Websaydkeenna, linkiga **Kontakt** wuxuu keenayaa oo kaliya fariin khalad ah.“ Bogga kontakt waa in laga heli karaa `http://192.168.0.20/kontakt.html`. Hel khaladka oo hagaaji.

<details>
<summary>Tilmaan 1: Maxay server-ku sheegayaan?</summary>

Fur bogga kontakt adigoo isticmaalaya Browser ama `curl -v`. Status code-ka noocee ah ayaa soo laabanaya? Bogga khaladku wuxuu kaloo muujiyaa meesha server-ku faylka ka raadiyay.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Tilmaan 2: Xaggee bay faylashu yaalaan?</summary>

Web server-ku wuxuu bixiyaa oo kaliya faylasha ku jira **Document Root** (*Document Root*) — tan waxaad ka arki kartaa app-ka **HTTP-Server**. Ka eeg **Server**-ka gudaha Terminal adigoo isticmaalaya `ls` waxa halkaas yaal, oo bogga kontakt sidoo kale ka raadi `/home`.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Tilmaan 3: Faylka rar meel kale</summary>

Adigoo isticmaalaya `mv` ayaad faylka u rartaa meel kale: `mv <Quelle> <Zielordner>`.
</details>

:::task
title: Bogga kontakt ma la hagaajiyay?
Hubi in bogga kontakt uu ku jiro Document Root ee web server-ka.
check: fileExists(2, "/var/www/kontakt.html")
:::

:::tip Qodob muhiim ah
**404** macnaheedu waa: web server-ku wuu shaqaynayaa oo la gaari karaa, laakiin **faylka** la codsaday ma jiro waddadan (path). Server-ku wuxuu ka raadiyaa oo kaliya **Document Root** (*Document Root*, halkan `/var/www`). Is barbar dhig: haddii server-ku warbixin ku celin waayo ama uu ku jawaabo `RST`, dhibaatadu way ka qoto dheertahay — waxay ku jirtaa network-ka ama adeegga laftiisa (cutubka 5.3.2).
:::
