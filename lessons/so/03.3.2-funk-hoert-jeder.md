Hadda waxaa WLAN-ka `Schule` ku jira laba tablet: **Anna** (`192.168.0.30`) iyo **Ben** (`192.168.0.31`).

Xusuuso cutubka 3.1: Markii Anna uu switch-ka ka ping-gareeyay Ben, Clara waxba kama ogaan — switch-ku wuxuu ping-yada u diray port-ka Ben oo keliya. Ma WLAN-kana sidaas oo kale ayay tahay? Ka fikir wax yar, maxaad filaysaa?

## Tijaabada

1. U beddel habka :fa-play: **Run** oo **Anna** ka ping-garee Schul-PC (`ping 192.168.0.10`).
2. U beddel habka :fa-magnifying-glass: **Raad** oo adigoo isticmaalaya **+** u fur trace **Ben** ah, connection `wlan0`.

- [x] Dhammaan packets-ka u dhexeeya Anna iyo Schul-PC, oo ay ku jiraan ping-yada
- [ ] Waxba, ping-yadu Ben khusayn maayaan

## Maxaa ka dambeeya

Mowjadaha raadiyaha waxay ku fidaan dhinac kasta. Sidaas darteed wixii Anna diro waxay gaadhaan qalab *kasta* oo u dhow — ma aha Access Point-ka oo keliya. Raadiyuhu waa **shared medium**. Tablet-ka Ben caadi ahaan wuxuu iska indho-tiraa wax kasta oo aan isaga loo socon. Laakiin qof kasta oo *doonaya* inuu duubo, wuu akhrisan karaa wax walba.

| | Switch (fiilo) | WLAN (raadiyo) |
|---|---|---|
| Yaa helaya unicast? | port-ka receiver-ka oo keliya | qalab kasta oo kala dhex jira xadka daboolka |
| Ma akhrisan karaa qof saddexaad? | caadi ahaan maya | haa, si fudud |

:::tip Qodob muhiim ah
Raadiyuhu waa **shared medium**: wax kasta oo WLAN-ka lagu diro waxaa heli kara qalab kasta oo xadka daboolka ku jira. Sidaas darteed WLAN waa in la **encrypt-gareeyaa** (maanta waa **WPA2** ama **WPA3**) — kolkaas shisheeyuhu xogta way heli karaan, laakiin ma akhrisan karaan.
:::

:::note
BeaverTracer ma simulate-gareeyo WLAN encryption — sidaas darteed halkan wax walba waad akhrisan kartaa. Sida encryption u shaqeeyo waxaad ku baran doontaa cutubka 8.
:::
