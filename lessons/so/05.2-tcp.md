**Transmission Control Protocol** (**TCP**) waxa uu ku sugan yahay ku dhawaad wax kasta oo internetka ku gaadhaya si aamin ah: bogagga webka, e-mail-ada, soo dejinta faylasha (downloads), iyo ilaalinta fog ee SSH.

Boggaga soo socda waxaad indha indheyn doontaa hal TCP connection min bilowga ilaa dhammaadka — adigoo adigu naftaada ka raadinaya halka TCP uu ku fuliyo connection setup, gudbinta aamin ah iyo xidhitaanka connection-ka.
