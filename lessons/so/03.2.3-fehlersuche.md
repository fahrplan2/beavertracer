Kadib ddayactir iyo isbeddel lagu sameeyay dhammaadka toddobaadka, iskuulku wuxuu Isniinta soo wargeliyay: „**Ben** si lama filaan ah ayuu u gaadhayaa kombiyuutarrada maamulka — laakiin mar dambe ma gaadho Anna.“ Raadi khaladaadka oo hagaaji.

## Network plan

Sidan ayuu network-ku *u baahan yahay* inuu u habaysan yahay:

| Qalab | Switch | Port | VLAN | IP address |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sekretariat | Switch EG | 1 | 10 | `192.168.0.10` |
| Anna | Switch EG | 2 | 20 | `192.168.0.20` |
| Schulleitung | Switch OG | 1 | 10 | `192.168.0.11` |
| Ben | Switch OG | 2 | 20 | `192.168.0.21` |

Port 8 waa trunk-ga labada switch: mode `tagged`, Allowed VLANs `10,20`.

<details>
<summary>Talo 1: Xagee ka bilaabaa?</summary>

Labada switch ka fur Tab-ka **VLAN** oo sadar kasta ku shaqaysii network plan-ka: PVID-ga port-yada PC-yada iyo Allowed VLANs ee trunk-ga.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Talo 2: Waxaa jira laba khalad</summary>

Hal khalad ayaa sharxaya sababta Ben u gaadhayo maamulka. Kan kale wuxuu sharxayaa sababta uusan Anna u gaadhi karin — labaduba waxay ku jiraan **Switch OG**.
</details>

## Maxaa dhacay?

:::quiz fill
Ben wuxuu ku jiray VLAN {10} halkii uu ka ahaan lahaa VLAN {20}. Intaa ka dib, trunk-ga switch-ka {OG|Obergeschoss} ma uusan oggolaan VLAN {20}.
:::

:::evaluate
Hubi jawaabaha
:::

:::tip Qodob muhiim ah
Khaladaadka caadiga ah ee VLAN: port leh **PVID khaldan** (qalabku wuxuu galaa VLAN khaldan) iyo **trunk aan oggolaanayn VLAN** (qalabyada isla VLAN-ka ah ee switch-yo kala duwan ku jira isma gaadhaan). Sidaa darteed mar walba hubi *labada dhinac* ee trunk-ga.
:::
