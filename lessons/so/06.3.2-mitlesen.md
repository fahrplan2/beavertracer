Sida HTTP oo kale, SMTP waa **textprotokoll** (protocol qoraal ah). Eeg wax kasta oo xadhigga ku maraya qaab qoraal caadi ah (clear text).

## Tijaabada

1. Marka aad ku jirto :fa-play: **Run** (Habka Socodsiinta), sida bogga hore u dir mail ka timaada **Anna** una socota `ben@biber-mail.de`.
2. Marka aad ku jirto :fa-magnifying-glass: **Raad** (Habka Raadka), fur trace u gaar ah **Anna**. Tiirka **Info** wuxuu muujinayaa wada hadalka SMTP: xariiqyada leh `C:` waxaa soo diraya client-ka, kuwa leh `S:` waxaa soo diraya server-ka.

:::quiz short
Cinwaankee ayaa ka dambeeya `MAIL FROM:`?
= anna@beaver-schule.de
= <anna@beaver-schule.de>
:::

:::quiz short
Kuwa ka dambeeya `RCPT TO:`-na?
= ben@biber-mail.de
= <ben@biber-mail.de>
:::

:::quiz mc
Koodhka lambarka ah ee uu server-ku soo diro marka uu amar aqbalo waa kuwee?
- [x] `250`
- [ ] `200`
- [ ] `404`
:::

:::evaluate
Hubi jawaabaha
:::

## Passwordka-na?

Kahor `MAIL FROM`, barnaamijka Anna wuxuu isku diiwaangeliyaa `AUTH PLAIN` iyo xarriiq xaraf ah sida `AGFubmEAZ2VoZWlt`. Tani waxay u egtahay mid la siray (encrypted), laakiin ma aha: waa **Base64** oo keliya, nidaam qoris (encoding) oo qof walba isla markiiba dib u akhrin karo — gudaheeda waxaa ku jira `anna` iyo passwordkeeda `geheim`.

Hadda ka qaad boostada **Ben** (**Soo qaad**) oo fur trace u gaar ah **Ben**. Marka lagu soo qaado IMAP, si ka cad ayaa loo arkaa:

:::quiz short
Passwordkee ayuu barnaamijka email-ka ee Ben u diraa server-ka qaab qoraal caadi ah? (Eeg xariiqda leh `LOGIN`.)
= biber123
:::

:::evaluate
Hubi jawaabta
:::

:::warning
Haddii aan encryption jirin, qof kasta oo duubta data traffic-ga wuu akhrin karaa **nuxurka, sender-ka, receiver-ka iyo passwordyada**. Sidaa darteed maanta waxaa la isticmaalaa noocyo la siray (tusaale ahaan SMTP oo leh STARTTLS, IMAPS oo ku shaqeeya port 993). Sida ay u shaqeyso waxaad ka baran doontaa cutubka 8.
:::

:::tip Qodob muhiim ah
**SMTP**, **POP3** iyo **IMAP** waa textprotocols: amarrada sida `MAIL FROM`, `RCPT TO`, `DATA` (SMTP) ama `LOGIN` (IMAP) iyo jawaabaha waa la akhrin karaa. Haddii aan encryption jirin, **passwordyada** sidoo kale waxay xadhigga maraan qaab qoraal caadi ah (ama kaliya Base64 lagu qoray).
:::
