# Qaynuunnadaada ugu horreeya

:::sim
url=/sims/lesson-9.btsim
:::

Hadda waa adigii ku soo laabtay. Firewall-ku wali wax walba wuu ogolyahay inay ka gudbaan. Hubi in Internet-ka laga gaari karo oo kaliya **Webseite (80)** iyo **Mail-Empfang (25)** ee server-ka dugsiga. **SSH (22)**, **POP3 (110)** iyo **IMAP (143)** waa in aan Internet-ka laga gaari karin.

## Gelinta qaynuunnada

1. Guji **Firewall**-ka. Siyaasadda caadiga ah (default policy) hadda ha ahaato **Alle zulassen**.
2. Tirtir qaynuunka jira (wax walba ayuu ogolyahay) oo adigoo isticmaalaya **+ Regel hinzufügen** u samee qaynuun port kasta oo la xiraayo:
    - **Richtung** A → B (laga soo galo Internet-ka)
    - **Protokoll** TCP
    - **Ziel-Port** tusaale `22`
    - **Aktion** Verwerfen
3. Ka hubi **Internet-PC** adigoo isticmaalaya `nmap 198.51.100.10` in ports-ka saxda ah oo keliya ay furan yihiin.

:::task
title: Web iyo Mail oo keliya ayaa laga gaari karaa banaanka?
Waxay hubisaa in server-ka dugsiga laga gaari karo Internet-ka oo kaliya port 80 iyo 25 — iyo in Lehrer-PC uu weli mail soo qaadan karo oo Internet-ka daalacan karo. Simulation-ku waa inuu u socdaa Habka Socodsiinta.
check: tcpOpen(1, "198.51.100.10", 80)
check: tcpOpen(1, "198.51.100.10", 25)
check: tcpFails(1, "198.51.100.10", 22)
check: tcpFails(1, "198.51.100.10", 110)
check: tcpFails(1, "198.51.100.10", 143)
check: tcpOpen(8, "198.51.100.10", 110)
check: tcpOpen(8, "203.0.113.80", 80)
:::

:::quiz mc
Lehrer-PC wuxuu weli POP3 (110) ku soo qaadan karaa mail-ka server-ka dugsiga. Maxay firewall-ku u carqaladeyn?
- [ ] Maxaa yeelay firewall-ku wuxuu garanayaa macallimiinta
- [x] Maxaa yeelay packets-kiisu ma dhex maraan firewall-ka — Lehrer-PC iyo server-ka dugsiga waxay ku xiran yihiin isla switch-ka ka dambeeya firewall-ka
- [ ] Maxaa yeelay POP3 waa protocol ammaan ah
:::

:::note
Firewall-ku wuxuu ilaaliyaa oo kaliya traffic-ga **dhexmara** isaga. Wixii ka dhaca gudaha network-ka dugsiga ma arko. Kombuyuutar ay cudur gaadhay oo ku jira network-ka dugsiga ayaa weli weeraari kara server-ka dugsiga — taas awgeed ayaa qaybta DMZ looga hadlayaa.
:::

:::tip Qodob muhiim ah
Adeeg kasta oo banaanka laga gaari karin, qaynuun leh **Richtung**, **Protokoll** iyo **Ziel-Port** ayaa xirta marinka. In qaynuunnadu shaqeynayaan waxaa banaanka looga hubiyaa **Portscan**.
:::
