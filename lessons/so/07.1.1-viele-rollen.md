## Waxa ku jira home router-ka

| Doorka ku jira home router-ka | Waxa uu sameeyo | Meesha aad ka taqaanid |
|---|---|---|
| **Switch** | wuxuu isku xidhaa qalabka ku jira godadka LAN | Cutubka 3.1 |
| **Access Point** | wuxuu telefoonnada, tablet-yada iyo laptop-yada ku xidhaa network-ka isagoo adeegsanaya WLAN | Cutubka 3.3 |
| **Router** | wuxuu isku xidhaa home network-ka iyo network-ka provider-ka — waa **gateway** | Cutubka 4.3 |
| **DHCP Server** | wuxuu qaybiyaa cinwaannada, gateway iyo DNS server | Cutubka 6.4 |
| **DNS** | wuxuu hor u gudbiyaa codsiyada magacyada ama wuxuu sheegaa DNS server-ka provider-ka | Cutubka 6.2 |
| **NAT** | wuxuu qalabka oo dhan ku xidhaa internet-ka isagoo adeegsanaya **hal** cinwaan oo dadweyne | cusub — Qaybta 7.2 |

Isku xidhka **WAN** (*Wide Area Network*) wuxuu u horseedaa provider-ka, isku xidhyada **LAN** iyo WLAN-kuna waxay u horseedaan home network-ka. Home router-ro badan waxay kaloo ka kooban yihiin **modem** — wuxuu u tarjumaa xogta xariiqda (DSL, fiilada telefishanka, fiber). BeaverTracer taas ma simuleeyo; halkan isku xidhka WAN wuxuu si toos ah ugu xiran yahay network-ka provider-ka.

:::tip Qodob muhiim ah
**Home router** wuxuu isku daraa dhowr qalab: **switch**, **WLAN Access Point**, **router** (gateway-ga loo maro internet-ka), **DHCP Server** iyo **DNS forwarding** — iyo weliba **NAT**. Isku xidhka **WAN** wuxuu u horseedaa provider-ka, isku xidhyada **LAN** iyo WLAN-kuna waxay u horseedaan home network-ka.
:::
