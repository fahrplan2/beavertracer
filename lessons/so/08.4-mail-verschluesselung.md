# E-mail loo diro si sir ah (encrypted)

:::sim
url=/sims/lesson-8.4.btsim
:::

Qaybta 6.3.2 waxaad ku aragtay in SMTP iyo POP3 ay sender-ka, receiver-ka, nuxurka iyo xitaa password-ka ku gudbiyaan si akhrin karaa xadhigga dhexdiisa. Iyadoo la adeegsanayo TLS, tan waa la xaqiijin karaa sida HTTP oo kale. E-mail-ka waxaa u jira laba hab:

| | **Implicit TLS** | **STARTTLS** |
|---|---|---|
| Habka socodka | Xidhiidhku wuxuu si sir ah u noqdaa ilbiriqsigii ugu horreeyay. | Xidhiidhku wuxuu ku bilaabmaa si aan sir ahayn; client-ku wuxuu diraa amarka `STARTTLS`, ka dib ayaa la sir-yeelaa. |
| Ports | port u gaar ah: **SMTPS 465**, **POP3S 995**, **IMAPS 993** | port-yada caadiga ah, tusaale ahaan SMTP 25 ama 587 |

Xarafka „S“ ee SMTPS, POP3S iyo IMAPS wuxuu — sida HTTPS oo kale — u taagan yahay *Secure*.

## Hore: wax walba waa la akhriyi karaa

Ku jira **Mailserver Schule** TLS horeba waa loo habeeyay: wuxuu leeyahay certificate loogu talagalay `mail.beaver-schule.de`, wuxuuna intaas dheer dhegaystaa port-yada 465, 995 iyo 993. Laakiin barnaamijka e-mail-ka ee Anna wuxuu weli isticmaalaa port-yada aan sir ahayn ee 25 iyo 110.

1. U beddel :fa-play: habka **Run**, ku fur barnaamijka **E-Mail** ee **Anna** oo u dir e-mail `ben@beaver-schule.de`.
2. Kadib guji **Soo qaad**.
3. Ku fur trace u gaar ah **Anna** habka :fa-magnifying-glass: **Raad**.

## U beddelidda port-yada sir ah

Ku fur **Settings** ee barnaamijka e-mail-ka ee Anna:

- **Soo gelaya:** port `995` iyo calaamadi **TLS**
- **Baxaya (SMTP):** port `465` iyo sir-yeelid **Implicit TLS**

Kaydi, mar kale e-mail dir oo mar kale soo qaad. Eeg trace-ka (filter `tls`).

:::tip Tijaabi
U beddel dirista port `25` iyadoo sir-yeelidda la dhigayo **STARTTLS** oo e-mail dir. Trace-ka waxaad ku arkaysaa sida xidhiidhku horta ugu bilaabmayo si aan sir ahayn — server-ku wuxuu jawaabtiisa `EHLO` ku soo bandhigaa `STARTTLS` —, oo kaliya amarka `STARTTLS` ka dib ayuu raacaa TLS handshake-ku.
:::

## Waxa sir-yeelidda gaadiidku aysan ilaalin

TLS wuxuu sir yeelaa kaliya **jidka** u dhexeeya laba kombiyuutar: laga bilaabo Anna ilaa mail server-ka iyo — haddii labada mail server ay taageeraan — laga bilaabo mail server ilaa mail server. Laakiin **mail server-yada laftooda** e-mail-ku wuxuu ku jiraa qoraal fudud oo aan sir ahayn. Qof kasta oo geli kara mail server wuxuu akhrin karaa dhammaan e-mail-ada ku jira.

Haddii kaliya receiver-ku uu akhriyi karo e-mail, waa in si **dhammaad-ilaa-dhammaad** (end-to-end) loo sir-yeelo: sender-ku wuxuu u sir-yeelaa furaha dadweynaha ee receiver-ka, receiver-kuna keliya ayaa mar kale furi kara. E-mail-ka waxaa taas u jira habab **S/MIME** iyo **OpenPGP**; messenger-yada sida Signal ama WhatsApp tan si toos ah ayay u sameeyaan.

:::tip Qodob muhiim ah
E-mail-ka waxaa si sir ah loogu gudbiyaa **TLS** — ama **implicit TLS** oo port u gaar ah leh (**SMTPS 465**, **POP3S 995**, **IMAPS 993**) ama **STARTTLS** oo port-yada caadiga ah ku shaqeeya. Tani waa **sir-yeelidda gaadiidka**: waxay ilaalisaa jidka, laakiin ma ilaaliso e-mail-ka ku jira server-ka. Taas waxaa loo baahan yahay **sir-yeelid dhammaad-ilaa-dhammaad**.
:::
