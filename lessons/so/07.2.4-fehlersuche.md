# Cilad-raadin: Shalay waa shaqaynaysay

:::sim
url=/sims/lesson-7.2.4.btsim
:::

Shalay web server-ka guriga waxaa laga heli jiray dibadda — port forward `TCP 80 → 192.168.178.10:80` waa la dejiyay. Kadib korontada oo go'day subaxnimadii maanta, Clara hadda ma gaari karto bogga. Raadi khaladka oo si joogto ah u hagaaji.

<details>
<summary>Tilmaan 1: Xagee ayuu xeerku ku tilmaamayaa?</summary>

Is bar bar dhig LAN IP-ga ku jira **Port FW** ee home router-ka iyo cinwaanka uu **Heim-Webserver** hadda dhab ahaan haysto (:fa-gear: **IP Settings**).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Tilmaan 2: Maxaa uu u haystaa cinwaan kale?</summary>

Ma **DHCP** baa lagu dejiyay Heim-Webserver-ka? Haddii ay sidaas tahay, markasta oo dib loo bilaabo wuxuu helayaa cinwaan bilaash ah oo kasta oo ka mid ah furanaha home router-ka (ee tab-ka **DHCP**). Sidee u hubin kartaa inuu mar walba helo isla cinwaanka?
</details>

:::task
title: Heim-Webserver ma mar kale ayaa laga heli karaa?
Hubi in Heim-Webserver-ku haysto cinwaankiisa go'an iyo in Clara ku gaadhayso port forward-ka. Simulation-ku waa inuu u socdaa Habka Socodsiinta.
check: ip(11, "192.168.178.10/32")
check: tcpOpen(12, "203.0.113.2", 80)
:::

:::tip Qodob muhiim ah
Aaladaha uu **port forward** ku tilmaamo (server-yada, printer-yada, kaameerooyinka) waxay u baahan yihiin **cinwaan go'an**: ama si static ah loo geliyay oo **ka baxsan DHCP range-ka**, ama loo dejiyay qoondayn go'an ee DHCP Server-ka router-ka. Haddii kale, dib-u-bilaabista ka dib waxaa laga yaabaa inay helaan cinwaan kale — xeerkuna waxba ma tilmaamayo.
:::
