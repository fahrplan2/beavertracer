# VLANs oo ku dhex jira laba switch: trunk-ga

:::sim
url=/sims/lesson-3.2.2.btsim
:::

Dugsigu wuxuu leeyahay laba dabaq, mid walbana switch ayuu leeyahay, waxaana isku xira fiilo ku xiran **Port 8**:

| Switch | Port 1 (VLAN 10, Verwaltung) | Port 2 (VLAN 20, Computerraum) |
|---|---|---|
| **Switch EG** (dabaqa hoose) | Sekretariat `192.168.0.10` | Anna `192.168.0.20` |
| **Switch OG** (dabaqa sare) | Schulleitung `192.168.0.11` | Ben `192.168.0.21` |

VLANs-ka ku jira ports-ka PC-yada hore ayaa loo habeeyey.

## Dhibaatada

U beddel :fa-play: habka **Habka Socodsiinta** oo ping ka samee **Anna** una dir Ben (`ping 192.168.0.21`). Labadooduba waxay ku jiraan VLAN 20 — haddana jawaab ma imaanayso.

Ka fiiri **VLAN** Tab-ka switch-ka safka **port 8**: wali waa `untagged` oo PVID-gu yahay `1`. Haddaba fiiladan waxaa ku mara VLAN 1 oo kaliya — ma aha VLAN 10 ama VLAN 20.

Hase yeeshee fiilada u dhexeysa labada switch waa inay ku maraan frames ka socda *labada* VLAN. Sidaas darteed switch-ku wuxuu frame kasta ku dhejiyaa warqad yar oo ay ku qoran tahay VLAN-ID: waa **VLAN-Tag**. Sidaas ayuu switch-ka dhinaca kale ku ogaadaa VLAN-ka uu frame-ku ka tirsan yahay.

## Habaynta trunk-ga

Tan ku samee **labada** switch:

1. Guji switch-ka oo fur **VLAN** Tab-ka.
2. Safka **port 8**, **Modus** u dhig `tagged` oo ku qor `10,20` meesha **Allowed VLANs**.
3. Guji **Apply**.

Kadib mar kale ping ka samee **Anna** una dir Ben — oo is-barbardhig u dir Schulleitung (`ping 192.168.0.11`).

- [ ] Anna wuxuu gaadhaa Ben iyo Schulleitung labadaba

:::

## VLAN-Tag-ga ku jira tracer-ka

U beddel :fa-magnifying-glass: habka **Raad** oo adigoo isticmaalaya **+** fur trace u gaar ah **Switch EG**, port **8** — taasi waa fiilada u dhexeysa labada switch. Guji mid ka mid ah ping-yada Anna (`Echo (ping) request`).

Protocol tree-ga waxaa ka soo baxay sadar cusub, si toos ah Ethernet hoos yimid: **802.1Q Virtual LAN**. Kaasi waa VLAN-Tag-ga.

:::quiz short
Which VLAN-ID ayaa ku jira tag-ga ping-ka Anna?
= 20
:::

Si aad isu barbardhigto, fur trace u gaar ah **Ben** (`eth0`) oo halkaas ku dhufo ping-kii oo kale.

:::quiz mc
Maxaad ka dhex aragtaa Ben?
- [ ] Halkaas waxaa yaal tag isku mid ah oo ID-giisu yahay 20
- [x] Halkaas sadarka 802.1Q ma jiro — switch-ku tag-ga ayuu mar kale ka saaray ka hor port-ka Ben
- [ ] Ping-ku Ben ma gaadho gabi ahaanba
:::

:::evaluate
Hubi jawaabaha
:::

:::tip Qodob muhiim ah
**Trunk** waa xiriir ay ku mari karaan frames ka socda VLANs badan — inta badan u dhexeeya laba switch. Sidaas darteed frame kasta wuxuu helaa **VLAN-Tag** ay ku jirto VLAN-ID (standard-ka **IEEE 802.1Q**). Ports-kaas waxaa loo yaqaan **tagged**. Port-ka **untagged** waxaa ku xiran qalab caadi ah; switch-ku wuxuu tag-ga ka saaraa ka hor intuusan frame-ka halkaas u dirin.
:::
