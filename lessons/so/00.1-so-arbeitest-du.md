# Sida aad ugu shaqayso koorsada

Koorsadan waxay ka dhacdaa gudaha BeaverTracer: dhanka bidix waxaad ka dhisaysaa oo ka daawanaysaa networks, dhanka midig ee **Daaqadda casharrada** waxaad ka akhrisanaysaa tilmaamaha.

## Bog-rogista

Adigoo isticmaalaya **Dib** iyo **Xiga** ayaad koorsada ku maraysaa bog bog. Adigoo isticmaalaya **Guudmar** (ama :fa-list: oo ku jira dusha Daaqadda casharrada) ayaad u gudbi kartaa cutub kasta.

## Bog kasta waxaa ku jira simulation ku habboon

Mar kasta oo aad bog beddesho, BeaverTracer wuxuu si toos ah u soo shubaa simulation-ka bogga ku saabsan — sidaas darteed had iyo jeer waxaad ka bilaabaysaa xaalad shaqeynaysa.

:::warning
Wixii aad bog ku dhisto ama ku badasho, bogga xiga ma joogaan. Tani waa ula kac: bog kastaa wuxuu ku bilaabmaa xaalad bilow ah oo nadiif ah.
:::

- Adigoo isticmaalaya :fa-rotate-left: oo ku jira dusha Daaqadda casharrada, simulation-ka waxaad dib ugu celisaa bilowga bogga hadda jira — waa ku fiican tahay haddii aad lumisay.
- Haddii aad rabto inaad simulation lafteeda ku sii dhisto, ku dhufo :fa-file-import: **Qaadash** goobta qalabka (toolbar). Daaqadda casharrada way xirmaysaa, simulation-kuna waa kaaga.

## Kaydinta iyo soo shubidda

Simulation waa hal fayl (`.btsim`) oo qeexaya network-kaaga oo dhan — qalab kasta, wayr kasta iyo habaynta ay leeyihiin. Inta Daaqadda casharrada furan tahay, badhamada :fa-file: **Cusub**, :fa-file-arrow-up: **Soo shub** iyo :fa-file-arrow-down: **Kaydi** waa qarsoon yihiin. Kadib markaad **Qaadash** gujiso (ama aad Daaqadda casharrada ku xirto **×**), waxaad ka heli doontaa goobta qalabka dhanka midig, agtooda badhamada habka — gudaha Habka Tafatirka.

## Sanduuqyo, quizzes iyo hawlo

:::goal
**Hadafka waxbarashada** — wuxuu ku jiraa bilowga qayb kasta: tan ayaad samayn kartaa kadib.
:::

:::tip Qodob muhiim ah
Wuxuu kooban ku soo koobayaa erayga farsamo ama xidhiidh muhiim ah — wuxuu ku fiican yahay barashada imtixaannada.
:::

- **Su'aalaha quiz-ka** si toos ah bogga ayaad uga jawaabaysaa, oo waxaad ku hubisaa badhanka hoose ku yaal. Ku dhawaad jawaab kasta waxaad ka akhrisan kartaa shaashaddaada haddii aad si fiican u fiirisid.
- **Hawlaha** leh badhanka **Hubi tasks** waxay ku eegaan simulation-kaaga in wax walba si sax ah loo habeeyey — tusaale ahaan in laba PC ay is gaari karaan.
