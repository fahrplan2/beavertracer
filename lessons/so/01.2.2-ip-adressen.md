# Bixinta IP address

:::sim
url=/sims/lesson-1.2.2.btsim
:::

Si labada qalab ay isu hadli karaan, waxay u baahan yihiin IP address ku jirta isla network-ka. Goobta shaqada waxaad ku aragtaa laba PC oo isku xiran — sida kuwii aad hadda gacantaada ku dhisay.

1. U gudub habka :fa-play: **Habka Socodsiinta** oo dhagsii **Computer 1** si aad u furto.
2. Halkaas ka fur :fa-gear: **IP Settings**. PC cusub wuxuu horeyba u leeyahay cinwaanka `192.168.0.10` iyo netmask `/24` — sidaas ayaad ku reeb kartaa.
3. Hadda fur **Computer 2**. Wuxuu leeyahay cinwaan *isku mid ah* `192.168.0.10` — taasi ma shaqayn karto, sababtoo ah cinwaan kasta wuxuu network-ka ku jiri karaa hal mar oo keliya. U beddel `192.168.0.11` (netmask `/24`).

:::tip Qodob muhiim ah
**IP address** (tusaale `192.168.0.10`) waa cinwaanka qalab ku jira network-ka — waxaa lagu barbardhigi karaa lambarka guriga. IP address kasta wuxuu network-ka ku jiri karaa **hal mar** oo keliya.
:::

:::task
title: Cinwaannada si sax ah ma loo bixiyay?
Ku sii jir Habka Socodsiinta oo hubi in labada PC ay leeyihiin cinwaanno ku haboon oo ay is gaari karaan.
check: ip(1, "192.168.0.10/32")
check: ip(3, "192.168.0.11/32")
check: pingOk(1, 3)
:::

:::note
Macnaha saxda ah ee `/24` iyo sida network loogu qaybiyo si habboon waxaan si faahfaahsan uga hadlaynaa cutubka 4. Hadda waxaa ku filan: labada cinwaan waa inay ku bilaabmaan `192.168.0.` oo aan la soo noqnoqon.
:::
