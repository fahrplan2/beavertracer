# Cilad-raadinta shabakadda dugsiga

:::sim
url=/sims/lesson-4.4.3.btsim
:::

Saaxiib shaqo ayaa dejiyay PC-yada shabakadda dugsiga — tan iyo markaas, inta u dhaxaysa qaybaha waxba kuma shaqeeyaan. Waa sidaan shabakaddu *u dhici lahayd*:

| Qalab | IP address | Prefix length | Gateway |
|---|---|---|---|
| PC Verwaltung | `192.168.10.2` | `/26` | `192.168.10.1` |
| PC Lehrerzimmer | `192.168.10.66` | `/26` | `192.168.10.65` |
| PC Computerraum | `192.168.10.130` | `/26` | `192.168.10.129` |

Hel khaladaadka oo hagaaji.

<details>
<summary>Talo 1: Xaggee ka bilaabaa?</summary>

PC kasta eeg :fa-gear: **IP Settings** oo u eeg sida ay ula mid yihiin network plan. Khalad kasta isla markiiba lama arko.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Talo 2: Maxaa saxda ah oo khaldan?</summary>

PC kasta xisaabi: Address-kiisu subnet-kee ayuu runtii ku jiraa? Ma waxay noqon kartaa network address ama broadcast address? Netmask-kuna ma sax baa?
</details>

:::task
title: Shabakadda dugsiga ma la hagaajiyay?
Hubi address-yada saddexda PC iyo in ay isu gaari karaan router-ka dhexdiisa. Simulation-ku waa inuu u socdaa Habka Socodsiinta.
check: ip(9, "192.168.10.2/32")
check: ip(11, "192.168.10.66/32")
check: ip(13, "192.168.10.130/32")
check: pingOk(9, 11)
check: pingOk(9, 13)
check: pingOk(11, 13)
:::

## Maxaa dhacay?

:::quiz match
PC Verwaltung -> Address-ka .70 wuxuu ku jiraa subnet-ka Lehrerzimmer — gateway-ga .1 halkaas lagama gaari karo
PC Lehrerzimmer -> Netmask-ku wuxuu ahaa /24 halkii uu ka ahaan lahaa /26
PC Computerraum -> Address-ka .191 waa broadcast address-ka subnet-ka
:::

:::evaluate
Hubi isku-xirka
:::

:::tip Qodob muhiim ah
Khaladaadka caadiga ah ee subnetting: **address ka baxsan subnet-kaaga** (markaas gateway-gana lagama gaari karo), **netmask khaldan**, iyo **network address ama broadcast address oo loo isticmaalay address qalab**. Digniin: IP Settings qiimayaashan khaldan way aqbalaan digniin la'aan — xisaabta adigaa isku hubinaya.
:::
