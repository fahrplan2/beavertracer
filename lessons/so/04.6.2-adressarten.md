# Noocyada ciwaannada ugu muhiimsan

Marka la eego IPv6, bilowga ciwaanka (address) ayaad ka garan kartaa waxa loogu talagalay:

| Nooca ciwaanka | laga garto | loogu talagalay |
|---|---|---|
| **Link-Local** | `fe80::` | Wuxuu shaqeeyaa kaliya **network-gaaga maxalliga ah**. Qalab kasta wuxuu naftiisa si toos ah u siiyaa ciwaankan — router weligiis uma gudbiyo. |
| **Global Unicast** | `2…` ama `3…` (`2000::/3`) | Ciwaan adduunka oo dhan gaar ah, lagu gaari karo internet-ka. Prefix-ka waxaa laga helaa bixiyaha internet-ka. |
| **Unique Local** | `fd…` (`fc00::/7`) | Loogu talagalay network-yada gaarka ah, waxaa lala barbar dhigi karaa `192.168.x.x` ee IPv4. |
| **Loopback** | `::1` | Qalabkaaga lafteeda — sida `127.0.0.1` ee IPv4. |
| **Multicast** | `ff…` | Waxaa loo diraa **koox** qalab ah, tusaale `ff02::1` = dhammaan qalabka ku jira network-ga maxalliga ah. |

:::note
Aagga `2001:db8::/32` waxaa loo kaydiyay tusaalooyin iyo agabka waxbarashada — marna ma ka muuqdo internet-ka dhabta ah. Sidaas daraaddeed ayaad ku aragtaa koorsadan.
:::

Isbeddel muhiim ah: **IPv6 mar dambe ma yaqaan broadcast.** Wixii IPv4 ku ahaa broadcast loo diro qof kasta, IPv6 wuxuu ku qabtaa ciwaannada **multicast** — fariintu waxay u socotaa kaliya qalabka xiiseynaya kooxdaas.

:::tip Qodob muhiim ah
Qalab IPv6 ah kasta wuxuu leeyahay ugu yaraan hal **ciwaan Link-Local** (`fe80::…`) oo loogu talagalay network-giisa, badanaana wuxuu leeyahay sidoo kale **ciwaan global ah** oo loogu talagalay internet-ka. Halkii uu ka isticmaali lahaa broadcast, IPv6 wuxuu isticmaalaa **multicast** (`ff…`).
:::
