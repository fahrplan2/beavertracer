# BeaverTracer — Koorsada barashada

Ku soo dhawaaw **koorsada isdhexgalka ah** ee BeaverTracer. Halkan waxaad tallaabo tallaabo ku baran doontaa sida networks u shaqeeyaan — si toos ah browser-kaaga ku jirta, adiga oo isticmaalaya protocols dhab ah iyo packet analyzer ku dhex dhisan oo ku salaysan tignoolajiyada Wireshark.

**Ma u baahnid aqoon hore.** Cashar kasta wuxuu ku bilaabmaa tijaabo yar: wax ayaad dhisaysaa, waxaad fiirisaa wuxuu ka dhacayo network-ga, kaddibna waad baranaysaa ereyada farsamo ee la xiriira.

## Waxa kugu sugaya

1. **Hordhac** — interface-ka, network-gaaga ugu horreeya, soo qaadista bog web ah oo gadaal ka eegis lagu sameeyay
2. **Moodellada** — sida packet loo dhisay iyo sida protocols loogu kala saaro layers (ISO/OSI, TCP/IP)
3. **Switches iyo networks maxalli ah** — sida switch u ogaado meesha packet u socdo; VLANs iyo WLAN
4. **Routers iyo IP addresses** — netmask, gateway iyo **subnetting**: u qaybinta networks si macquul ah
5. **Transport layer** — ports, TCP iyo UDP
6. **Codsiyada** — DHCP, DNS, HTTP iyo e-mail
7. **Home network** — router, NAT iyo wax kasta oo ka yimid cutubyadii hore oo la isu geeyay
8. **Amniga** — yaa akhrin kara, encryption, certificates iyo HTTPS
9. **Firewall** — yaa network-ga gelaya iyo yaan gelin
10. **VPN** — isku xirka laba goob si ammaan ah
