# Ping oo la socodsiiyo oo lagu raadraaco Tracer-ka

:::sim
url=/sims/lesson-1.2.3.btsim
:::

Labada PC hadda waxay haystaan cinwaannada `192.168.0.10` iyo `192.168.0.11`. Waa waqtigii imtixaanka koowaad: **Ping** waxay u suurtagelisaa PC inuu codsi yar u diro mid kale, kadibna uu sugo jawaabta.

## Ping oo la socodsiiyo

U beddel habka :fa-play: **Run**. Gudaha **Computer 1** fur :fa-terminal: **Terminal** oo ku qor:

```
$ ping 192.168.0.11
```

Haddii PC-ga kale jawaabo, waxaad arkaysaa mid ka mid ah xariijimaha packet kasta oo la diray oo leh waqtiga jawaabta — kaasi waa ping-gaagii ugu horreeyay ee guulaystay!

:::tip Qodob muhiim ah
**Ping** waxay hubisaa in qalab kale la gaari karo: waxay dirtaa codsi, waxayna cabbirtaa inta jawaabtu qaadanayso.
:::

## Lagu raadraaco Tracer-ka

Hadda u beddel habka :fa-magnifying-glass: **Raad**. Halkan wax weli lagama arko — gudaha bidix ee sare, agtiisa **Traces:**, guji **+**, dooro **Computer 1**, kadibna dooro dekediisa `eth0`.

Hadda waxaad arkaysaa liis ka kooban dhammaan packets-ka dekedan ku dhex maray — oo ay ku jiraan ping-yadii hadda ka hor. Guji mid ka mid ah packets-ka: hoosta waxaad ku arkaysaa dhismihiisa saxda ah, layer ka layer.

:::tip Qodob muhiim ah
Xogta network-ka kuma dirto mid dhan, laakiin waxaa loo dirayaa cutub yaryar oo la yiraahdo **packets**. Packet kasta wuxuu wataa sender-ka, receiver-ka iyo qayb ka mid ah xogta.
:::

:::tip
Ping had iyo jeer wuxuu ka kooban yahay codsi (Echo Request) iyo jawaab (Echo Reply) — ma ka heli kartaa labada dhinac tiirka **Info**?
:::

## Yaa weydiinaya ka hor ping-ga koowaad?

Aadka u sarraysa liiska, xataa ka hor ping-ga koowaad, waxaa yaal laba packet oo leh protocol-ka **ARP**. Akhri tiirkooda **Info** — halkaas waxaa ku qoran wax u eg:

```
Who has 192.168.0.11? Tell 192.168.0.10
192.168.0.11 is at aa:fd:9f:a5:d2:89
```

Computer 1 wuxuu garanayay IP address-ka PC-ga kale, laakiin weli ma garanayn **cinwaanka hardware-ka** (*MAC address* — kaarka network-ka kasta wuxuu leeyahay midkiisa gaarka ah). Isagoo aan haysan, waxba ma diri karo. Sidaa darteed marka hore wuxuu ku qaylinayaa network-ka: *„Yaa haysta 192.168.0.11? U sheeg 192.168.0.10!“* PC-ga la raadinayo wuxuu ku jawaabayaa cinwaanka hardware-kiisa. Taasi waa shaqada **ARP** si buuxda.

Kadib, Computer 1 wuxuu xasuustaa jawaabta — sidaa darteed ARP wuxuu ka muuqdaa kaliya ka hor ping-ga koowaad, ma aha ka hor mid kasta.

:::tip Qodob muhiim ah
**ARP** wuxuu IP address la yaqaan u helaa cinwaanka hardware-ka ee ku habboon (**MAC address**): PC-gu wuxuu weydiiyaa dhammaan network-ka, kaliya qalabka la raadinayo ayaa jawaaba.
:::

:::quiz mc
Yaa Computer 1 u diraa packet-ka ARP ee ugu horreeya? Eeg tiirka **Ziel**.
- [ ] Kaliya PC-ga kale
- [x] Dhammaan qalabka ku jira network-ka (broadcast)
- [ ] Server ku jira internet-ka
:::

:::quiz mc
Maxay Computer 1 ka baraneysaa jawaabta ARP?
- [ ] IP address-ka PC-ga kale
- [x] Cinwaanka hardware-ka (MAC address) ee PC-ga kale
- [ ] Magaca PC-ga kale
:::
