# Cilad-raadin: digniinaha certificate

:::sim
url=/sims/lesson-8.3.4.btsim
:::

Shabakadda iskuulka waxaa ku jira saddex web server oo kale: **Mensa**, **Bibliothek** iyo **Sport-AG**. Saddexduba waxay isticmaalaan HTTPS — in kasta oo ay sidaas tahay, Browser-ka **Client-PC** wuxuu mid walba ka tusaa digniin. Client-PC wuxuu aamminaa **Schul-CA**.

## Baadh

U gudub habka :fa-play: **Habka Socodsiinta** oo **Client-PC** ka fur kala daraaz:

- `https://mensa.beaver-schule.de/`
- `https://bibliothek.beaver-schule.de/`
- `https://sport.beaver-schule.de/`

Mar kasta akhri digniinta iyo macluumaadka certificate-ka (Betreff, Aussteller, Gültig bis).

:::quiz match
mensa.beaver-schule.de -> Certificate-ku wuu dhacay (muddadiisu way dhammaatay)
bibliothek.beaver-schule.de -> Certificate-ku waa self-signed, client-kuna ma aamino
sport.beaver-schule.de -> Certificate-ka waxaa loo bixiyay magac kale
:::

## Hagaaji Mensa

Mensa waa ka ugu dhakhsaha badan in la hagaajiyo: certificate-keeda mar ayay Schul-CA ka bixisay — wuxuu kaliya dhacay. Si loo fududeeyo, Schul-CA halkan waxay si toos ah ugu jirtaa Mensa-server.

<details>
<summary>Tilmaam 1: Soo saar certificate cusub</summary>

Ka fur **mensa.beaver-schule.de** :fa-shield-halved: **Zertifikatverwaltung**. Tab-ka **Signieren** ku jira waxaad ka codsan kartaa Schul-CA inay certificate-ka jira **mar kale saxiixdo** — sidaas darteed wuxuu helayaa muddo cusub oo shaqaynaysa. Ku kaydi magac fayl cusub, tusaale ahaan `mensa-neu.cert.json`.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Tilmaam 2: Isticmaal certificate-ka cusub</summary>

Gudaha **HTTP-Server** ee Mensa: **Stoppen**, tab-ka **HTTPS** ka dooro certificate-ka cusub (waxaad ku garan kartaa magaca faylka), **Verwenden**, **Bilow**.
</details>

:::warning
**„Trotzdem fortfahren“** marka digniin certificate ah timaado ma aha fikrad wanaagsan inta badan. Sababtoo ah si sax ah ayay u eegi lahayd haddii weerarqaade uu ku siiyo certificate-kiisa. Waxyaabaha oo kale ah ayaa khuseeya `curl -k`: waxay damisaa hubinta certificate-ka — waa wax faa'iido leh imtixaanka, laakiin khatar ayay tahay isticmaalka dhabta ah.
:::

:::tip Qodob muhiim ah
Browser-ku wuxuu digniin bixiyaa marka certificate-ku **dhacay**, marka uu **garan waayo CA-ga** bixiyay (tusaale ahaan **self-signed** certificates), ama marka **magaca** ku jira certificate-ka uusan ku habboonayn cinwaanka la furay. Xalku waa mar walba certificate ku habboon oo shaqaynaya oo ka yimid CA la aamini karo — ma aha in digniinta la iska xidho.
:::
