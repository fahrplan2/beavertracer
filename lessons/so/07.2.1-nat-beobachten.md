## Waxa ka dambeeya

Home router-ku wuxuu packet kasta oo internet-ka u socda ku beddelaa cinwaanka sender-ka ee gaarka ah **cinwaanka WAN ee dadweynaha** oo isaga u gaar ah — badanaanna sidoo kale port-ka sender-ka. Web server-ku wuxuu u arkaa sidii codsigu uga yimid home router-ka. Marka jawaabtu soo laabato, home router-ku mar kale ayuu cinwaanka beddelaa oo packet-ka u gudbiyaa PC-ga.

Sidaas ayaa home network-ka oo dhan loogu geli karaa internet-ka hal cinwaan oo dadweyne — qalabka internet-kuna weligood ma arkaan cinwaannada gaarka ah.

## Jidka ku wajahan internet-ka

Ku qor **PC**-ga :fa-terminal: **Terminal**:

```
$ traceroute 192.0.2.80
```

:::quiz mc

- [x] `192.168.178.1` — cinwaanka LAN ee home router-ka
- [ ] `203.0.113.2` — cinwaankiisa WAN

:::tip Qodob muhiim ah
**NAT** (*Network Address Translation*) wuxuu marka loo socdo internet-ka cinwaanka sender-ka **ee gaarka ah** ee qalab ku beddelaa **cinwaanka WAN ee dadweynaha** ee router-ka — jawaabaha marka ay soo laabanayaanna si ka guraysa. Sidaas ayaa dhammaan qalabka home network-ka ay u wadaagaan **hal** cinwaan IPv4 oo dadweyne. Tani waxay badbaadisaa cinwaannada waxayna ka qarisaa cinwaannada gaarka ah internet-ka.
:::
