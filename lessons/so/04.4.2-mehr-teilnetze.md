# Subnets badan, octets kale

Midabada sida qaybtii hore: [[n|network part]] · [[e|extension]] · [[h|host part]]

## Subnets ama hosts — waa inaad doorataa

Extension bit kasta oo dheeraad ah wuxuu labanlaabaa tirada subnets — wuxuuna kala badhaa cabbirka subnet kasta. Haddii network-ku yahay `/24`, waxay u egtahay sidan:

| Subnets | Prefix length | Netmask | Cinwaannada subnet kasta | Hosts subnet kasta |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | `/25` | `255.255.255.128` | 128 | 126 |
| 4 | `/26` | `255.255.255.192` | 64 | 62 |
| 8 | `/27` | `255.255.255.224` | 32 | 30 |
| 16 | `/28` | `255.255.255.240` | 16 | 14 |
| 32 | `/29` | `255.255.255.248` | 8 | 6 |
| 64 | `/30` | `255.255.255.252` | 4 | 2 |

Marka aad qorshaynayso, sidaas darteed waa inaad labadaba hubisaa: Ma jiraan **subnets ku filan** — oo miyay **ku filan yihiin** qalab kasta?

:::quiz mc
Network `/24` ah waa in loo kala qaybiyaa 8 subnets. Aagga mid ah waxaa ku jira 40 qalab. Ma ku filan tahay?
- [ ] Haa, subnet kasta wuxuu leeyahay 62 hosts
- [x] Maya, subnet kasta wuxuu leeyahay 30 hosts oo keliya

## Jid-gaab: block size

Haddii aad fahantay habka binary system, waxaa sidoo kale lagu samayn karaa si dhakhso badan. Tirada cinwaannada subnet kasta waxaa la yiraahdaa **block size**. Waxay ka timaadaa tirada host bits ee octet-ka uu xadku ku yaal — ama si fudud sidan: **256 laga jaray masked octet-ka**:

- `/26` → mask `255.255.255.192` → 256 − 192 = **64** → subnets waxay ka bilaabmaan .0, .64, .128, .192
- `/27` → mask `255.255.255.224` → 256 − 224 = **32** → subnets waxay ka bilaabmaan .0, .32, .64, .96 …

## Tusaale: sideed subnets

`192.168.42.0/24` oo loo kala qaybiyay **sideed** subnets: 3 bit (2³ = 8), `/24` wuxuu noqonayaa `/27`, mask `255.255.255.224`, block size 32.

| 2 | [[e|001]] | `192.168.42.32` | `192.168.42.63` | `.33` – `.62` |
| 3 | [[e|010]] | `192.168.42.64` | `192.168.42.95` | `.65` – `.94` |
| 4 | [[e|011]] | `192.168.42.96` | `192.168.42.127` | `.97` – `.126` |
| 5 | [[e|100]] | `192.168.42.128` | `192.168.42.159` | `.129` – `.158` |

## Marka xadku ku jiro octet kale

Habku wuxuu u shaqeeyaa si isku mid ah marka network-ka asalka ahi uu ka weyn yahay `/24`. Markaas extension-ku wuxuu ku jiraa octet kale — **octets kasta oo ka dambeeya** wuxuu gebi ahaan ka tirsan yahay host part.

### Tusaale: `172.20.0.0/16` oo loo kala qaybiyay afar subnets

2 extension bits, `/16` wuxuu noqonayaa `/18` (mask `255.255.192.0`). Extension-ku hadda wuxuu ku yaal bilowga octet-ka **saddexaad**, octet-ka afraadna dhammaantiis waa host part:

<pre class="bits-block">
[[n|172.20.]] [[e|E E]] [[h|H H H H H H . H H H H H H H H]]
</pre>

| Subnet | EE | Network address (host bits 0) | Broadcast address (host bits 1) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [[e|00]] | `172.20.0.0` | `172.20.63.255` |
| 2 | [[e|01]] | `172.20.64.0` | `172.20.127.255` |
| 3 | [[e|10]] | `172.20.128.0` | `172.20.191.255` |
| 4 | [[e|11]] | `172.20.192.0` | `172.20.255.255` |

Feeraw: broadcast address-ka subnet 1 waa `172.20.63.255` — maaha `172.20.63.0`. Octet-ka afraad bits-kiisa oo dhan waa host bits, waxaana loo dhigaa 1 broadcast address-ka. Subnet kasta wuxuu leeyahay 2¹⁴ − 2 = 16 382 hosts.

### Tusaale: `10.16.0.0/12` oo loo kala qaybiyay sideed subnets

3 extension bits, `/12` wuxuu noqonayaa `/15` (mask `255.254.0.0`). Markan xadku wuxuu ku yaal octet-ka **labaad**. Lambarka 16 waa binary `0001 0000`; afarta bit ee ugu horreeya horeyba waxay uga tirsan yihiin network part, ka dibna waxaa soo socda saddexda extension bit iyo hal host bit:

<pre class="bits-block">
[[n|10. 0 0 0 1]] [[e|E E E]] [[h|H . H H H H H H H H . H H H H H H H H]]
</pre>

| Subnet | EEE | octet-ka labaad (network address) | Network address | Broadcast address |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [[e|000]] | [[n|0001]] [[e|000]] [[h|0]] = 16 | `10.16.0.0` | `10.17.255.255` |
| 2 | [[e|001]] | [[n|0001]] [[e|001]] [[h|0]] = 18 | `10.18.0.0` | `10.19.255.255` |
| 3 | [[e|010]] | [[n|0001]] [[e|010]] [[h|0]] = 20 | `10.20.0.0` | `10.21.255.255` |
| 4 | [[e|011]] | [[n|0001]] [[e|011]] [[h|0]] = 22 | `10.22.0.0` | `10.23.255.255` |
| 5 | [[e|100]] | [[n|0001]] [[e|100]] [[h|0]] = 24 | `10.24.0.0` | `10.25.255.255` |
| 6 | [[e|101]] | [[n|0001]] [[e|101]] [[h|0]] = 26 | `10.26.0.0` | `10.27.255.255` |
| 7 | [[e|110]] | [[n|0001]] [[e|110]] [[h|0]] = 28 | `10.28.0.0` | `10.29.255.255` |
| 8 | [[e|111]] | [[n|0001]] [[e|111]] [[h|0]] = 30 | `10.30.0.0` | `10.31.255.255` |

:::warning Khaladaad caadi ah
- **Prefix si khaldan loo kordhiyay:** 4 subnets waxaa lagu daraa 2 bit, maaha 4 — waxa muhiimka ah waa tirada bits, maaha tirada networks.
- **Octet khaldan:** Extension-ku wuxuu si toos ah ugu xidhmaa network part-kii hore. Haddii network-ku yahay `/16`, wuxuu ku jiraa octet-ka saddexaad, maaha afraad.
- **Octets dambe la illaaway:** Haddii xadku aanu ku jirin octet-ka ugu dambeeya, octets-ka soo socda dhammaantood waa host part — sidaas darteed broadcast address-ka waa `255`.
- **Network address ama broadcast address in qalab la siiyo:** Aagga host wuxuu ka bilaabmaa hal cinwaan *kadib* network address, wuxuuna ku dhammaadaa hal cinwaan *ka hor* broadcast address.
:::

`172.20.0.0/16` waxaa loo kala qaybiyay 4 subnets. Subnet-ka labaad wuxuu leeyahay network address {172.20.64.0} iyo broadcast address {172.20.127.255}. Host-ka koowaad ee subnet-kan waa {172.20.64.1}.
:::
