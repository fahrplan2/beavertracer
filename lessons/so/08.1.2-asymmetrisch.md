# Sirdoonka asymmetric (asymmetric encryption)

## Fikradda: quful furan

Qiyaas in Ben uu qaybiyo **quful furan** dhammaan dadka doonaya inay isaga qoraan. **Furaha** ku habboon isagu naftiisa ayuu u haystaa.

1. Anna waxay fariinteeda gelisaa sanduuq, kadibna waxay ku xidhaa mid ka mid ah quful-yada Ben.
2. Hadda wixii ka dambeeya qofna sanduuqa ma furi karo — xataa Anna lafteedu.
3. Ben keliya ayaa furahiisa ku furi kara mar kale.

Qofkasta ayaa quful heli kara; haysashadiisu kuma caawiso furista. Sidaas ayaa saxda ah ee **asymmetric encryption** u shaqayso: Ka qaybgale kasta wuxuu leeyahay **key pair**:

| | **Public Key** (*furaha dadweynaha*) | **Private Key** (*furaha gaarka ah*) |
|---|---|---|
| waxay le'eg tahay | quful furan | furaha qufulka |
| yaa ogsoon? | **qof kasta** — waa la faafin karaa | **mulkiilaha keliya** — weligiis kama baxo kombiyuutarkiisa |
| waxa loo isticmaalo? | fariimaha loo diro mulkiilaha **in la sirdoonyeeyo** (encrypt) | fariimahaas **in la furo** (decrypt) |

Labada furaha waxay xisaab ahaan isku xidhan yihiin: Wixii lagu sirdoonyeeyay public key, **waxaa lagu furi karaa** keliya private key-ga ku habboon. Sidaas ayaa dhibaatada is-weydaarsiga furaha la xalliyay: Ben wuxuu public key-giisa si furan u diri karaa — qofka dhegeysanaya wax uu ku furo ma heli karo.

## Maxaa private key-ga lagu xisaabin karin?

Public key waxaa laga xisaabiyaa private key. Dhanka kale ma suurtagasho dhab ahaan. Habka ugu caansan, **RSA** (loogu magac daray Rivest, Shamir iyo Adleman, 1977), wuxuu ku salaysan yahay in **tirooyin waaweyn aad u adag tahay in loo kala jejebiyo prime factors-kooda**: Isku dhufashada laba tiro oo prime ah oo aad u weyn waa degdeg aad u badan — laakiin ka heli kara labada factor ee wax-soo-saarka leh in ka badan 600 lambar, kombiyuutar maanta jira ma sameyn karo.

:::note
Marka laga reebo RSA, waxaa jira habab ku saleysan **elliptic curves** (loo soo gaabiyo **EC**, tusaale **ECDHE** iyo **ECDSA**). Waxay ku salaysan yihiin dhibaato xisaabeed oo kale oo adag, laakiin waxay u baahan yihiin furayaal aad u gaaban. Mabda'u — public key iyo private key — isku mid ayuu yahay. Isku xidhka HTTPS ee casriga ah iyo sidoo kale BeaverTracer waxay isticmaalaan elliptic curves; soo gaabinahan ayaad mar kale ku arki doontaa Tracer-ka.
:::

## Dhibaatada: gaabis

Habka asymmetric wuxuu u baahan yahay xisaab aad u culus, wuxuuna ka **kun jeer gaabis badan yahay** AES. In bog web oo dhan ama muuqaal lagu sirdoonyeeyo waxay noqon lahayd mid aad u gaabis ah. Sida labadaba loo isku daro, waxaad ku arki doontaa qaybta **Hybrid encryption**.

:::tip Qodob muhiim ah
Marka la isticmaalayo **asymmetric encryption**, qof kasta wuxuu leeyahay **key pair**: **Public key-ga** qof kasta ayaa ogaan kara; waxaa lagu sirdoonyeeyaa fariimaha loo diro mulkiilaha. Keliya **private key-ga** mulkiilaha ayaa mar kale furi kara. Habab sida **RSA** waxay ku salaysan yihiin in tirooyin waaweyn aanay u fududayn in loo kala jejebiyo prime factors-kooda. Habka asymmetric wuxuu xalliyaa dhibaatada is-weydaarsiga furaha, laakiin waa gaabis.
:::
