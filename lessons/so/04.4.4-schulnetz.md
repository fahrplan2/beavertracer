# U qaybi network-ka dugsiga

:::sim
url=/sims/lesson-4.4.2.btsim
:::

Dugsigu wuxuu leeyahay address range `192.168.10.0/24`. Verwaltung, Lehrerzimmer iyo Computerraum mid walba waxaa loo baahan yahay inuu helo subnet u gaar ah. Kuwaasi waa saddex network — laakiin subnets isku cabbir ah waxay jiraan oo kaliya iyadoo la adeegsanayo awoodaha 2 (powers of two). Sidaa darteed waxaad u qaybisaa **afar** subnet; midda afraad waxay u hartaa kaydka (tusaale ahaan WLAN mustaqbalka lagu dari doono).

## Tallaabada 2: Deji

Router-ku horeba waa loo dejiyay: wuxuu subnet kasta ku leeyahay **address-ka ugu horreeya ee la isticmaali karo** (`192.168.10.1`, `192.168.10.65` iyo `192.168.10.129`).

Hadda deji saddexda PC. PC kasta wuxuu helayaa **address-ka labaad ee la isticmaali karo** ee subnet-kiisa, prefix length-ka ku habboon, iyo gateway ahaan address-ka router-ka ee subnet-kiisa.

1. U gudub habka :fa-play: **Habka Socodsiinta**.
2. Marka aad ku jirto **PC Verwaltung**, fur :fa-gear: **IP Settings**, geli **IP**, **Präfixlänge** iyo **Gateway**, kadibna riix **Apply**.
3. Isku sidaas samee **PC Lehrerzimmer** iyo **PC Computerraum**.
4. Adeegso `ping` si aad u tijaabiso inay PC-yadu isu gaari karaan.
