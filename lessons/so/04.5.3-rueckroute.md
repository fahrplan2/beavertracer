# Cilad-raadin: routeka dib-u-celinta

:::sim
url=/sims/lesson-4.5.3.btsim
:::

Qaab-dhismeedka waa isaga kii hore: Firma A — Router A — Zwischennetz — Router B — Firma B. Maamulaha ayaa **Router A** ku daray route-ka loo maro Firma B (`172.16.0.0/24` iyada oo loo marayo `10.0.0.2`). Si kastaba ha ahaatee, Anna waxay soo sheegaysaa: Clara ma jawaabayso.

Raadi qaladka oo hagaaji.

<details>
<summary>Tilmaan 1: Ping-ga Anna ma gaadhayaa meesha loo diray?</summary>

Anna ka ping u dir Clara, oo fur trace u dhaca **Clara**. Ping-ku ma gaadhay iyada — oo ma jawaabtay?
</details>

<details>
<summary>Tilmaan 2: traceroute intee ayuu gaadhayaa?</summary>

Anna ku qor `traceroute 172.16.0.10`. Router kee ayay jawaabuhu ka soo noqonayaan — router-kee se ayaa u baahan inuu jawaabaha Clara u gudbiyo Anna?
</details>

<details>
<summary>Tilmaan 3: Yaa yaqaan network kee?</summary>

Eeg routing table-ka **Router B**. Ma ogyahay halka packets-ka loogu talagalay `192.168.0.0/24` aadaan?
</details>

:::task
title: Isku xidhka ma la saxay?
Hubi in Router B uu yaqaan jidka loo maro Firma A iyo in Anna ay gaadhayso Clara. Simulation-ku waa inuu u socdaa Habka Socodsiinta.
check: hasRoute(5, "192.168.0.0/24")
check: pingOk(9, 11)
:::

:::tip Qodob muhiim ah
Xidhiidh kasta wuxuu u baahan yahay **jid tegitaan iyo jid soo noqosho**: router kasta oo jidka ku jira waa inuu aqaaniyaa *labada* network — network-ka bartilmaameedka iyo network-ka sender-ka. **Return route** maqan waa mid ka mid ah khaladaadka routing ee ugu badan. Taxaddar: `traceroute` wuxuu muujiyaa oo kaliya halka jawaabuhu ka joogsadaan — cilada waxay kaloo ku jirtaa karaa jidka soo noqoshada.
:::
