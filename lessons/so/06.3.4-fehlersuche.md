# Cilad-raadin: Email-ku ma gaadho

:::sim
url=/sims/lesson-6.3.4.btsim
:::

Anna waxay soo sheegtay: „Ma awoodo inaan Ben u diro mail!“ Sidan ayaa loo *habeeyay* inuu u dhisnaado:

| Maxay tahay | Waa inuu noqdaa |
|---|---|
| Annas Postausgang (SMTP) | `mail.beaver-schule.de`, Port `25` |
| MX-Eintrag von `biber-mail.de` | `mail.biber-mail.de` (Priorität 10) |

Raadi khaladaadka oo hagaaji. Kadib Anna ka dir mail `ben@biber-mail.de` — hawshu waxay hubisaa inuu gaadhay mailbox-ka Ben. (Gudbinta u dhexeysa mail server-yada waxay qaadan kartaa dhowr ilbiriqsi.)

<details>
<summary>Tilmaam 1: Maxay leedahay barnaamijka email-ka Anna?</summary>

Isku day inaad mail dirto oo eeg **Protokoll** Tab. Maxaa server-ku ka jawaabayaa — ma u egtahay SMTP?
</details>

<details>
<summary>Tilmaam 2: Mail-ku ma u soo laabtay sidii farriin khalad ah?</summary>

Ka soo qaad mail-ka Anna oo akhri farriinta ka timid `MAILER-DAEMON`. Maxaa keenay in gaarsiintu fashilanto? Isticmaal `nslookup -type=MX biber-mail.de` kombuyuutarka Anna si aad u hubiso in DNS server-ku yaqaan mail server-ka mas'uulka ah.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Tilmaam 3: Halkee lagu geliyaa tan?</summary>

Postausgang-ka waxaad ku dejisaa app-ka **E-Mail** ee Anna, hoosta **Einstellungen**. MX-Einträge waxaad ka helaysaa **DNS-Server** gudahiisa, Tab-ka **MX**.
</details>

:::task
title: Mail ma gaadhay?
Waxay hubisaa in mail ku jiro mailbox-ka Ben ee mail server-ka Biber.
check: fileExists(5, "/var/mail/ben.mbox")
:::

:::tip Qodob muhiim ah
Khaladaadka caadiga ah ee email-ka: **server ama port** khaldan oo ku jira barnaamijka email-ka (SMTP wuxuu ku shaqeeyaa port 25, POP3 port 110, IMAP port 143) iyo **MX-Eintrag** maqan ama khaldan oo ku jira DNS. Haddii mail server uusan mail gaarsiin karin, wuxuu u celiyaa sender-ka isagoo la socda farriin khalad ah (**Bounce**).
:::
