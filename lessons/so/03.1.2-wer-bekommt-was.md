# Yaa helaya packet kee?

:::sim
url=/sims/lesson-3.1.2.btsim
:::

Hadda Anna, Ben iyo Clara waxay ku xiran yihiin switch-ka (ports 1, 2 iyo 3). Marka Anna packet u dirto Ben: ma Ben keliya ayaa helaya — mise Clara sidoo kale?

Ka fikir wax yar maxaad filaysaa. Kadib isku day.

## Tijaabada

1. U gudub habka :fa-play: ** Run** (Habka Socodsiinta), ka fur :fa-terminal: **Terminal** **Anna** oo ping u dir Ben:
   ```
   $ ping 192.168.0.11
   ```
2. U gudub habka :fa-magnifying-glass: **Raad** oo adigoo isticmaalaya **+** u fur trace **Clara**, port `eth0`.
3. Adigoo isticmaalaya **+** u fur trace labaad **Ben**, port `eth0`. Kor ka dhex, waxaad u rogi kartaa labada trace.

## Waxa ka dambeeya

ARP request-ku wuxuu hadafkiisu yahay **Broadcast** — waxaa loo diray *dhammaan*. Sidaas darteed switch-ku wuxuu u gudbiyay port kasta, oo uu ku jiro Clara. Ping-yada, si ka duwan, waxaa loo cinwaaniyay Ben keliya. Switch-ku wuxuu u diray port-ka Ben oo keliya — Clara waxba kama ogaan.

:::tip Qodob muhiim ah
**Broadcast** wuxuu loo موجه yahay dhammaan qalabka ku jira local network-ka — switch-ku wuxuu u qaybiyaa dhammaan ports-ka. **Unicast** wuxuu loo موجه yahay hal qalab oo keliya — switch-ku wuxuu u diraa port-ka uu qalabkaasi ku xiran yahay oo keliya.
:::

## Eeg gudaha switch-ka

Laakiin switch-ku sidee buu u ogaadaa port-ka Ben ku xiran yahay? Ku noqo habka :fa-play: **Run** (Habka Socodsiinta) oo guji **Switch**-ka. Gudaha Tab-ka **SAT** waxaad ka arkaysaa address table-kiisa oo leh laba column: **MAC** iyo **Port**.

:::quiz mc
Qalabkee ayaa ku jira address table-ka?
- [ ] Anna, Ben iyo Clara
- [x] Kaliya Anna iyo Ben
- [ ] Kaliya Ben
:::

:::quiz short
Port-kee ayuu Ben ku xiran yahay marka loo eego jadwalka? Tilmaan: MAC address-ka Ben waxaad ka heli kartaa tracer-ka, ARP reply-giisa `192.168.0.11 is at …`.
= 2
= port 2
:::

:::quiz mc
Maxaa Clara looga waayay jadwalka?
- [ ] Silkeeda waa jiray
- [x] Wali ma dirin hal packet oo keliya
- [ ] Switch-ku wuxuu keydiyaa ugu badnaan laba gelin
:::

:::evaluate
Hubi jawaabaha
:::

Switch-ku wuu **bartaa**: Frame kasta oo ku yimaada, wuxuu eegaa MAC address-ka **sender**-ka oo wuxuu xasuustaa port-ka qalabkaasi ku xiran yahay. Markii ARP request-ka Anna yimid, wuxuu qoray "Anna → Port 1", markii jawaabta Ben yimidna "Ben → Port 2". Clara waxba ma dirin — sidaas darteed wali ma garanayo.

:::tip Qodob muhiim ah
Switch-ku wuxuu **address table**-kiisa (**SAT**, *Source Address Table*) ku xasuustaa MAC address-ka qalab kasta iyo port-ka lagu gaari karo — wuxuu ka bartaa cinwaannada sender-ka. Haddii uu garanayo hadafka frame-ka, wuxuu u diraa port-kaas oo keliya. Haddii uusan garanayn (ama uu yahay broadcast), wuxuu u diraa dhammaan ports-ka.
:::
