# Certificates: Yaa leh furahan?

Marka la isticmaalo asymmetric encryption iyo signatures, wax walba waxay u muuqdaan kuwo la xalliyay: web server-ka bankigaaga wuxuu kuu soo diraa public key-giisa, markaas waa la sii socdaa. Laakiin sidee ku ogaanaysaa in public key-gaas **runtii uu bankiga leeyahay**?

Weeraryahan jidka isku xidhka kuu leexiya wuxuu si fudud kuugu soo diri karaa **public key-giisa u gaarka ah**, wuuna sheegan karaa inuu yahay bankiga. Browser-kaaga ayaa markaas si hoggaaminaya ula encrypt-gareyn doona weeraryahanka — kaasoo wax walba akhriyaya. Weeraryahan sidaas u dhexda gala waxaa la yiraahdaa **Man-in-the-Middle**.

## Certificate: aqoonsi loogu talagalay furayaasha

Nolosha dhabta ah waxaad muujisaa **kaarka aqoonsiga** si aad u caddayso cidda aad tahay. Kaarka aqoonsiga waa la rumaysan yahay sababtoo ah waxaa soo saaray **hay'ad la aamino** — maamulka magaalada — iyo sababtoo ah way adag tahay in la been-abuuro.

Shabakadda (network) gudaheeda, **certificate** ayaa howshaas qabta. Waxay ka kooban tahay, inta kale ka dhex, kuwan:

| Goob | Macne | Kaarka aqoonsiga |
|---|---|---|
| **Subject** (*Betreff*, inta badan `CN=…`) | Yaa leh certificate-ka? Web server-yada: magaca, tusaale `CN=www.biberbank.de` | magaca lahaanshaha |
| **Public key** | Furaha mulkiilaha | sawir |
| **Issuer** (*Aussteller*) | Yaa soo saaray certificate-ka? | hay'adda soo saartay |
| **Validity** | laga bilaabo … ilaa … | ansax ilaa |
| **Signature**-ka soo-saaraha | Wuxuu caddeeyaa in soo-saaruhu runtii sidan u soo saaray certificate-ka | shaabad, hologram |

`CN` waxay u taagan tahay *Common Name*.

Hay'adda soo saarta certificate-yada waxaa la yiraahdaa **Certificate Authority** (*Zertifizierungsstelle*, kooban **CA**). Kahor intaysan saxiixin certificate loogu talagalay `www.biberbank.de`, waxay hubisaa in codsadaha runtii leeyahay domain-kaas.

## Yaa browser-ku aamminaa?

Browser-kaagu wuxuu certificate kasta hubiyaa:

1. Ma run baa **signature**-ka CA? (Tan waxay u baahan tahay public key-ga CA.)
2. Ma weli **ansax** baa certificate-ku?
3. Ma la mid yahay **magaca** certificate-ka cinwaanka la furay?

Laakiin sidee browser-ku u yaqaan CA? Nidaam hawlgal kasta iyo browser kasta waxaa ku jira liis ku dhow 150 **root CA** oo **horay loo rakibay**. Certificate-yadooda waa **self-signed** — iyagaa is caddeeya; waa la aamminaa sababtoo ah waxay la yimaadaan software-ka.

Inta badan root CA si toos ah ma saxiixdo, laakiin waxay u saxiixdaa **intermediate CA** dhexdeeda. Sidaas ayaa ka dhalata **chain of trust** (silsilad kalsooni):

```
Root-CA (vorinstalliert, vertraut)
  └─ signiert → Zwischen-CA
                  └─ signiert → www.biberbank.de
```

Browser-ku wuxuu silsiladda ka raacaa hoos ilaa sare ilaa uu ka gaadho root CA uu aamminsan yahay. Haddii taasi guuldarraysato — ama haddii ansaxnimada ama magacu aysan is waafaqin —, wuxuu muujiyaa **digniin**.

:::quiz match
Subject (CN) -> Magaca uu certificate-ku u shaqeeyo
Issuer -> CA-ga saxiixay certificate-ka
Public key -> Waxaa loo isticmaalaa hubinta signature-yada server-ka
Root CA -> Browser-ka ayaa horay ugu rakiban oo waa bilowga chain of trust
:::

:::tip Qodob muhiim ah
**Certificate** wuxuu xaqiijiyaa in **public key** uu u dhigmo **magac** gaar ah. Waxaa saxiixa **Certificate Authority (CA)**. Browser-ku wuxuu aamminaa certificate haddii uu **chain of trust** dhexdeeda ku gaadho **root CA** horay loo rakibay, certificate-kuna yahay **ansax**, **magacuna** la mid yahay cinwaanka.
:::
